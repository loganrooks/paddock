# Vision

## What This Could Be

An unofficial, private-only fan-made party game inspired by GeoGuessr, centered first on Formula 1 grand prix places, race-weekend atmosphere, and circuit-adjacent knowledge.

The initial audience is not broad casual sports traffic. It is fans who have followed F1 for years, know the calendar, remember race moments, and can recognize the difference between shallow trivia and sport-specific texture.

The practical design posture for this discovery phase is:
- optimize for private play value, not public-release caution
- assume the game can use direct F1 terminology and higher-fidelity references if they make the play experience better
- keep a weaker public-safe fallback in sight, but do not let it dominate the concept

## Why This Is Interesting

Most F1 fan products skew toward one of three lanes:
- official content consumption
- fantasy, prediction, or stats-heavy dashboards
- trivia that is broad but not especially social

A strong opportunity may exist in the overlap between:
- place recognition
- memory of race-weekend rituals
- circuit and city identity
- strategy literacy
- rules and stewarding literacy
- argument and interpretation
- social competition
- party-game energy

Research so far suggests the clean whitespace is not "another schedule/stats companion." The more interesting gap is a social game that lets knowledgeable fans perform expertise under pressure.

## Design Intent

The project should aim to feel:
- socially alive rather than solitary
- legible to watch, not just fun to play
- authentic to knowledgeable fans
- flexible enough to grow beyond one core mode

## Non-Goals For This Phase

This document does not decide:
- the final product name
- the exact v1 feature set
- the business model
- the tech stack
- whether the long-term product stays narrowly location-based or expands into a broader party platform
- whether public-safe distribution ever matters

## Current Product Tension

There are at least three competing truths to preserve:
- the seed fantasy is "GeoGuessr for grand prix locations"
- the bigger ambition is "an F1-flavored party game platform"
- the product is private-only for now, so high-fidelity references may be acceptable for design even if they would complicate public release later

The discovery process needs to keep all three alive long enough to see which product shape has the strongest combination of originality, feasibility, and fan authenticity.

## Working Product Thesis

The most promising version of this project may be:
- a strong geography-and-circuit anchor mode
- layered clue ladders that reward deep fan understanding
- a host-screen-first party shell for couch and private-room play
- several adjacent modes that test memory, strategy, bluffing, and argument rather than geography alone

This is still a thesis, not a commitment.
