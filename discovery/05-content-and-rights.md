# Content And Rights

This file tracks source opportunities and practical boundaries for a private-only unofficial fan product.

## Current High-Level Assumption

The primary lane is:
- private-only
- personal use
- optimized for strong F1 feel rather than public-safe conservatism

A secondary lane is still worth preserving in case the project later becomes shareable:
- lighter dependence on official media and protected assets
- clearer source tracking
- more self-authored abstractions

## Content Source Opportunities

### Official Editorial And Venue Context

Useful for clue writing and content design:
- official race pages and circuit guides
- destination and fan-travel guides
- race weekend logistics and fanzone context
- official race guide app feature descriptions

Best use:
- derive clue ideas
- derive venue identity
- derive race-weekend texture

Avoid:
- copying text verbatim into the game
- treating official pages as an API

### Rules, Regulations, And Stewarding Material

Particularly strong for expert-fan modes:
- FIA sporting regulations
- 2026 driving standards guidelines
- penalty guidelines
- technical regulation changes

Best use:
- argument rounds
- stewarding verdict modes
- explanation-heavy clue ladders
- "what would the stewards do?" scenarios

### Strategy And Technical Context

Useful references:
- Pirelli weekend previews
- tyre compound notes
- track degradation patterns
- race-specific strategic tendencies

Best use:
- tyre and strategy modes
- difficulty scaling
- clue step 2 in three-step ladders

### History And Statistics

Useful references:
- StatsF1
- historical race editorials
- season retrospectives
- incident roundups

Best use:
- era-sensitive rounds
- history and memory modes
- bracket and debate modes

### Geography And Map Context

Useful references:
- Google Maps / Street View for prototype clue media
- OpenStreetMap-derived venue and city context
- Wikidata for structured metadata
- Wikimedia Commons for some reusable supporting imagery

Best use:
- location-anchor mode
- reveal maps
- nearby-landmark clue writing

## Private-Only Practical Boundary

For this discovery phase, the main question is not "is this release-safe?" It is:
- does the reference materially improve the game
- is it easy to prototype
- does it help the game feel like F1 to knowledgeable fans

That said, some material is still more fragile than others.

## Lower-Fragility Material Types

- original UI and original written clue content
- public geography and map context
- self-authored abstractions derived from public information
- rules and regulation interpretations
- strategy explanations
- venue atmosphere and city-character clues
- team and room mechanics that evoke race weekends without copying official media

## Higher-Fragility Material Types

- official logos and stylized branding
- official images, video, audio, and broadcast-like assets
- exact circuit outlines if later public sharing matters
- official timing/results datasets used in substantial or automated ways
- app naming that suggests endorsement or official affiliation

## Current Take

The most fertile content seam appears to be:
- venue identity
- race-weekend texture
- strategy literacy
- stewarding and rules literacy
- historical meaning

In other words: this product gets stronger as it moves from shallow recognition toward layered interpretation.
