## Notes

- Primary sources were prioritized.
- GeoGuessr's public support pages were Cloudflare-blocked from this environment during direct fetch, so the current official homepage and visible live app strings were used instead.
- GeoTastic and GeoGrid expose limited explanatory text on their public landing pages. For those products, some mode descriptions were inferred from official live UI strings inside their shipped app bundles.
- Design critiques and "what it leaves open" sections in `03-reference-designs.md` are explicitly interpretive, not direct claims from the source pages.
