# Open Questions

## Product Shape

- Is the best first product a tight geography game, or a broader party shell with one standout geography mode?
- How much should the product optimize for watchability versus pure player challenge?
- Is asynchronous play important early, or should early design bias toward live sessions?
- Should the first truly distinctive mode still be geography-first, or could the breakout mode actually be strategy, stewarding, or debate?
- How much should we formalize now versus leave open? The current bias should be toward shared vocabulary and examples, not locked design.

## Audience

- How punishing should the hardest difficulty be for very knowledgeable fans?
- Should the product teach newer fans indirectly, or stay proudly expert-first?
- Should the room expect shared background knowledge, or should every round include enough reveal structure that one expert can carry a team?

## Content

- Which clue families feel best for private-only play:
  - location and street view
  - circuit identity
  - race-weekend texture
  - strategy and tyres
  - regulation and stewarding
  - history and debate
- How much manual authoring is acceptable before we need tooling?
- Does the anchor mode need direct Street View-like media, or can strong text/photo/map clue ladders be enough?
- Which answer surfaces look promising enough to keep visible without committing to them:
  - circuit
  - venue complex
  - exact corner or section
  - approach context
  - composite answers like `circuit + corner`

## Social Design

- What mode structures best support both couch co-op and remote multiplayer without fragmenting the experience?
- Should the product include spectators and audience influence from the start?
- Should the shared-screen experience be the main event, with remote as a secondary view of the same room, or should remote fairness carry equal weight early?

## Product Identity

- Does the eventual product want one iconic mode plus expansions, or a pack-like structure of multiple equal modes?
- Which fantasy should the eventual name and framing emphasize:
  - grand prix geography
  - race-weekend mastery
  - stewarding and strategy
  - full F1 party night

## Feasibility

- Which prototype stack is the cleanest first move:
  - Google Street View static clues plus Google map reveals
  - open-map rendering plus curated media clues
  - no live map APIs during play, just authored clue cards and reveals
- Is a spreadsheet-backed content schema enough for v0, or do we immediately need an internal authoring UI?
- Do we want to design around host-screen plus phone controllers from the first commit, or prove the core loop solo first?
