## Task Type

Initial architecture research/planning.

## Scope

Investigate how a private-first niche product like Prix Guesser might become selectively more public over time, and what kinds of discovery, invitation, or growth patterns would fit that trajectory.

This should not be treated as growth-hacking research.

It is a product-trajectory and trust study:

- how small hobby products expand responsibly
- what kinds of discovery align with the product's social shape
- how invite loops, public surfaces, and selective openness affect the nature of the product

## Starting Context

Read the shared starting context from the orchestration brief first.

## Research Instructions

Explore questions such as:

- what is the difference between "friends-only", "selectively public", and "public product" in practice?
- what discovery loops are compatible with a product that begins as private and curated?
- how do room-code products, invite-driven products, or challenge-link products grow?
- how do products preserve trust when they are capacity constrained or still semi-experimental?
- how does the likely product shape affect discovery strategy?

You should also consider:

- whether different wrappers imply different discovery paths
- whether discoverability should differ between local-room, async challenge, and spectator/streamer surfaces
- how public transition interacts with moderation and hosting burden
