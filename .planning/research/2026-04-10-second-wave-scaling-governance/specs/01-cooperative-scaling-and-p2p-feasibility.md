## Research Instructions

Explore possibilities such as:

- pure centralized hosting
- centralized hosting plus voluntary peer assistance
- self-host-your-own-room models
- cooperative community-hosting models
- browser-to-browser or peer-assisted distribution ideas

But do not assume these are all equally viable.

You should attend to:

- NAT traversal and networking realities
- browser constraints
- cheating/trust implications
- authoritative room-state implications
- media serving implications
- operator/user complexity
- casual-user adoption friction
- whether any contribution model could be meaningfully optional rather than mandatory

If the research reveals that some candidate models are technically possible but socially or operationally absurd, say so clearly.
