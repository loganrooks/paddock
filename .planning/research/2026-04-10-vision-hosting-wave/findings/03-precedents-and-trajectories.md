# Lane 03: Precedents And Trajectories

## Question Space

- [CONFIRMED] Prix Guesser's current planning posture is "private-only F1 fan project," "host-screen-friendly social format," and "authored F1 rounds" rather than thin geography rounds; this lane therefore needs precedents that illuminate hosting transitions, social wrappers, authored-content burden, and watchability, not just map gameplay. [`PROJECT.md`](../../PROJECT.md), [`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md), [`REQUIREMENTS.md`](../../REQUIREMENTS.md)
- [INFERRED] The most useful precedent question is not "what product is closest?" but "which products solved one of Prix Guesser's hard problems without importing the wrong center of gravity?"
- [INFERRED] The hard problems visible from current planning are: low-friction join for private rooms, clean separation between live and async wrappers, authored-content sustainability, spectator/streamer friendliness, and a credible path from "friends-only" to broader hosting without immediately becoming a public ladder product. [`PROJECT.md`](../../PROJECT.md), [`CONVERGENCE.md`](../../audits/2026-04-08-pre-execution-review/CONVERGENCE.md), [`SYNTHESIS.md`](../../audits/2026-04-08-pre-execution-review/SYNTHESIS.md)

## Findings

### Useful Analogies

- [CONFIRMED] **GeoGuessr is a strong analogy for wrapper expansion around a geography-recognition core.** Official materials show GeoGuessr spanning solo/classic modes, Daily Challenge, private friend play, invite-only challenges, and Live Challenges inside "Play with Friends"; GeoGuessr's own Party Team job post says many players begin with an invite from a friend and that the browser-based social experience is a "critical entry point." [GeoGuessr Classic](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407923788305-What-is-Classic), [Daily Challenge](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017720198-What-is-the-Daily-Challenge), [Play with Friends](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407930336145-What-is-Play-with-Friends), [Live Challenges](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4477015980945-What-are-Live-Challenges), [Party Team job post](https://career.geoguessr.com/jobs/6950938-senior-product-manager-party-team)
- [INFERRED] The reusable lesson from GeoGuessr is not its scoring grammar; it is that a narrow core interaction can support multiple wrappers if the product treats invite-driven social entry as a first-class surface rather than an afterthought. [Play with Friends](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407930336145-What-is-Play-with-Friends), [Party Team job post](https://career.geoguessr.com/jobs/6950938-senior-product-manager-party-team)
- [INFERRED] GeoGuessr is especially relevant to Prix Guesser's possible **private-to-broader** transition because the official career site publicly frames GeoGuessr as having grown from a 2013 project into a company with 80+ staff, 100M+ players, expansion across web/apps, and live esports. That does not tell us how the transition worked internally, but it does confirm that the product now sustains multiple surfaces around the original loop. [GeoGuessr career site](https://career.geoguessr.com/), [GeoGuessr Party Team job post](https://career.geoguessr.com/jobs/6950938-senior-product-manager-party-team)
- [CONFIRMED] **Jackbox is the strongest analogy for host-screen-plus-phone-controller ritual design.** Official support says one player runs the game on a laptop/console/TV-compatible device, other players join via `jackbox.tv` in a browser, no account is required, and most games support an audience role beyond core players. [Getting started](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794771245975-How-do-I-get-started-playing-Jackbox-Games), [How to join](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794759479959-How-do-I-join-a-game), [Accounts/subscriptions](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794759347735-Do-games-require-an-account-or-subscription-to-play), [Player counts](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794756085015-How-many-players-can-join-each-game)
- [INFERRED] Jackbox is a valuable precedent for Prix Guesser because it proves that "watching" and "playing" do not need to be the same role. A room can be socially satisfying when a small number of active players, a larger audience, and a shared screen all coexist deliberately instead of the audience being accidental spillover. [Player counts](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794756085015-How-many-players-can-join-each-game), [Audience Kit for Twitch](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794775761047-How-do-I-use-the-Audience-Kit-extension-for-Twitch)
- [CONFIRMED] Jackbox also offers a strong precedent for **bounded UGC instead of open publishing**: custom episodes use share codes, stay constrained to specific games, and published episodes become non-editable; moderation tools include profanity filtering, passworded rooms, Twitch login, moderator review, and kick controls. [Custom episodes](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794763590167-Using-custom-or-user-created-episodes), [Moderation](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794773430295-How-does-Moderation-work), [Trolls/streaming safety](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794764378519-What-if-I-encounter-trolls-while-playing-Jackbox-Games-titles)
- [CONFIRMED] **Kahoot is a useful analogy for operational clarity around live vs async wrappers.** Official support documents a live-hosted mode with PIN/link/QR entry and a separate assigned mode where learners progress at their own pace, receive deadlines, and continue without the host actively presenting in real time. [Host live](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039422694-How-to-host-a-live-kahoot), [Join](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039890713-Kahoot-join-code-how-to-join-a-Kahoot-game), [Assign](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039411334-How-to-assign-a-kahoot-in-web-platform)
- [INFERRED] Kahoot is useful to Prix Guesser not because it is quiz-like, but because it cleanly separates **host-paced session logic** from **self-paced challenge logic** while reusing the same content object and sharing infrastructure. [Host live](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039422694-How-to-host-a-live-kahoot), [Assign](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039411334-How-to-assign-a-kahoot-in-web-platform)
- [CONFIRMED] Kahoot also exposes two operationally valuable patterns Prix Guesser should notice early: hosts can preview both host and participant views before going live, and assigned sessions are effectively snapshotted because edits made after sharing the QR code do not propagate into assignments already in progress. [Preview](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/29968568026131-How-to-preview-a-kahoot), [Assign](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039411334-How-to-assign-a-kahoot-in-web-platform)

### Misleading Analogies

- [CONFIRMED] **GeoGuessr becomes a misleading master template if Prix Guesser copies its round contract.** GeoGuessr's official product description still centers random Street View placement, free exploration, and "the closer your guess, the more points you earn," which is materially different from Prix Guesser's authored-answer, reveal-rich F1 contract. [What is GeoGuessr?](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9486501024401-What-is-GeoGuessr), [`PROJECT.md`](../../PROJECT.md), [`REQUIREMENTS.md`](../../REQUIREMENTS.md)
- [INFERRED] Copying GeoGuessr too literally would bias Prix Guesser toward distance scoring, public competition, and procedural map breadth at exactly the moment the repo is trying to protect authored meaning, answer surfaces, and host-screen reveal grammar.
- [CONFIRMED] **Jackbox becomes a misleading architecture template if Prix Guesser forgets that Jackbox's host runtime is not browser-native.** Jackbox's official remote-play guidance still depends on screen sharing, streaming, or platform-specific remote-play tools; that is a useful transition pattern, but not the same as Prix Guesser's browser-first private-room ambition. [Remote play](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794770038423-Can-I-play-Jackbox-Games-remotely-), [Getting started](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794771245975-How-do-I-get-started-playing-Jackbox-Games)
- [INFERRED] If Prix Guesser treats Jackbox as "the answer," it may underinvest in persistent room state, reconnect semantics, and web deployment because Jackbox gets to offload part of the remote problem to Discord/Zoom/Twitch instead of solving it in-product.
- [CONFIRMED] **Kahoot is a misleading content analogy.** Its official docs emphasize correctness/speed options, player identifiers, presentation/lecture modes, and accessibility settings for question-and-answer flows; that is operationally useful, but it does not solve authored clue ladders, fuzzy recognition, or "why this was identifiable" reveals. [Host live](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039422694-How-to-host-a-live-kahoot), [Player identifier](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036178314), [Participant screens](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003197928-Kahoot-live-game-see-questions-on-participant-s-screen)

### Transferable Patterns

- [INFERRED] **Always offer three join surfaces together:** room code, direct link, and QR. GeoGuessr, Jackbox, and Kahoot all reduce friction through fast browser/device entry, and Kahoot's live flow makes the trio especially explicit. [Live Challenges](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4477015980945-What-are-Live-Challenges), [How to join](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794759479959-How-do-I-join-a-game), [Host live](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039422694-How-to-host-a-live-kahoot)
- [INFERRED] **Keep live and async wrappers separate in UX, but backed by the same content substrate.** GeoGuessr shows live friend play and async/daily structures coexisting; Kahoot shows live-hosted and assigned modes coexisting with different pacing rules. [Play with Friends](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407930336145-What-is-Play-with-Friends), [Daily Challenge](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017720198-What-is-the-Daily-Challenge), [Host live](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039422694-How-to-host-a-live-kahoot), [Assign](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039411334-How-to-assign-a-kahoot-in-web-platform)
- [CONFIRMED] **Snapshot shared content once a session starts.** Kahoot explicitly states that post-share edits do not affect assignments already in progress, and the Prix Guesser roadmap already wants session snapshots frozen at launch. [Assign](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039411334-How-to-assign-a-kahoot-in-web-platform), [`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md)
- [INFERRED] **Treat audience/spectator roles as designed roles, not edge cases.** Jackbox's explicit audience support and Twitch extension both show that passive or semi-active participants can be a designed layer of the product instead of noise around the main game. [Player counts](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794756085015-How-many-players-can-join-each-game), [Audience Kit for Twitch](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794775761047-How-do-I-use-the-Audience-Kit-extension-for-Twitch)
- [INFERRED] **Preview the host and participant surfaces before real play.** Kahoot's built-in split preview is a strong reminder that authored content and live pacing should be rehearsable before guests arrive. [Preview](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/29968568026131-How-to-preview-a-kahoot)
- [INFERRED] **Bound user-generated content behind narrow formats and moderation tools.** Jackbox's share-code custom episodes and staged moderation are a more realistic precedent than a broad open-pack marketplace for early Prix Guesser. [Custom episodes](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794763590167-Using-custom-or-user-created-episodes), [Moderation](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794773430295-How-does-Moderation-work)
- [INFERRED] **Keep identity lightweight until there is a real need for durable cross-session history.** Kahoot's player identifier is explicitly optional and report-oriented, while Jackbox mostly avoids accounts entirely for players. That lines up better with Prix Guesser's private-first posture than a heavy account system. [Player identifier](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036178314), [Accounts/subscriptions](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794759347735-Do-games-require-an-account-or-subscription-to-play), [`PROJECT.md`](../../PROJECT.md)

### Patterns Prix Guesser Should Probably Reject

- [INFERRED] **Reject "public ladder first" pressure.** GeoGuessr's public competitive surfaces work for GeoGuessr, but Prix Guesser's current roadmap, private-only posture, and authored-content burden suggest that chasing public ranked play early would likely pull effort away from hostability, reveal quality, and curated pack strength. [Daily Challenge](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017720198-What-is-the-Daily-Challenge), [GeoGuessr career site](https://career.geoguessr.com/), [`PROJECT.md`](../../PROJECT.md)
- [INFERRED] **Reject remote-play strategies that depend on third-party screen sharing as the core product answer.** Jackbox proves that this can work, but Prix Guesser's own roadmap is already investing in authoritative rooms and reconnect, which implies a browser room should eventually be the product, not a workaround. [Remote play](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794770038423-Can-I-play-Jackbox-Games-remotely-), [`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md)
- [INFERRED] **Reject fully open UGC publishing before content operations exist.** Jackbox's custom content works because it is bounded; GeoGuessr's community maps exist inside a much larger product and moderation/community context. Prix Guesser does not yet have the ops, moderation, or scale to absorb open publishing safely. [Where can I find maps to play?](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017613357-Where-can-I-find-maps-to-play), [Custom episodes](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794763590167-Using-custom-or-user-created-episodes)
- [CONFIRMED] **Reject fragile rejoin semantics as acceptable.** Kahoot's live-host article still warns that some rejoin flows may require a new nickname and score reset, while Prix Guesser already has a dedicated reconnect-and-durability phase. That is a useful cautionary precedent, not a quality bar to imitate. [Host live](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039422694-How-to-host-a-live-kahoot), [`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md)
- [INFERRED] **Reject the idea that host-screen and controller can be the same UI scaled up or down.** Jackbox and Kahoot both imply a differentiated host/player split, and the existing audit work in this repo already flags watchability as load-bearing. [Getting started](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794771245975-How-do-I-get-started-playing-Jackbox-Games), [Preview](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/29968568026131-How-to-preview-a-kahoot), [`CONVERGENCE.md`](../../audits/2026-04-08-pre-execution-review/CONVERGENCE.md)

### Potential Innovation Space

- [INFERRED] **Broadcast-style reveal design is the clearest whitespace.** GeoGuessr is strong on geography challenge, Jackbox is strong on party ritual, and Kahoot is strong on host/session operations, but none of them appear centered on authored sports-context reveals where the explanation is part of the payoff rather than post-round admin. [`REQUIREMENTS.md`](../../REQUIREMENTS.md), [What is GeoGuessr?](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9486501024401-What-is-GeoGuessr), [Getting started](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794771245975-How-do-I-get-started-playing-Jackbox-Games), [Host live](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039422694-How-to-host-a-live-kahoot)
- [INFERRED] **A hybrid local/hosted model could be a meaningful differentiation.** The repo already wants private rooms and later hosted transition paths; a product that is excellent on one laptop/TV plus phones, but can also move into browser-hosted remote rooms without rethinking the content model, occupies a space between Jackbox's packaged-host model and GeoGuessr/Kahoot's more fully web-service models. [`PROJECT.md`](../../PROJECT.md), [`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md), [Remote play](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794770038423-Can-I-play-Jackbox-Games-remotely-), [Host live](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039422694-How-to-host-a-live-kahoot)
- [INFERRED] **Trusted-circle curation may beat open community publishing.** Jackbox suggests bounded share codes can work; Prix Guesser could plausibly support semi-private pack sharing, visible authorship, and replayable reveal craft without immediately opening a marketplace or moderation nightmare. [Custom episodes](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794763590167-Using-custom-or-user-created-episodes)
- [HYPOTHESIS] **A community-supported model is more plausible if it funds curation, hosting, and transparent growth instead of promising mass public competition.** I did not find a clean primary-source precedent in this pass for the exact funding model, so this should be treated as a strategic hypothesis, not a researched conclusion.

## Viable Paths

- [INFERRED] **Path A: Host-screen-first private rooms, then async challenges.** This path leans hardest on Jackbox for room ritual and Kahoot for later self-paced wrappers. It fits the current roadmap best and minimizes early public-platform pressure. [`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md), [Getting started](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794771245975-How-do-I-get-started-playing-Jackbox-Games), [Assign](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039411334-How-to-assign-a-kahoot-in-web-platform)
- [INFERRED] **Path B: Geography-core service with stronger social/browser breadth.** This path leans more on GeoGuessr: live friend play, invite loops, daily or challenge wrappers, and eventual larger-scale competitive/community surfaces. It is viable, but it risks pulling Prix Guesser toward thin location gameplay unless the authored round contract remains dominant. [Play with Friends](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407930336145-What-is-Play-with-Friends), [Daily Challenge](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017720198-What-is-the-Daily-Challenge), [`PROJECT.md`](../../PROJECT.md)
- [INFERRED] **Path C: Curated F1 party platform with bounded creator input.** This path keeps the current v1 anchor mode, then adds trusted custom packs, additional wrappers, and spectator tools before any open platform move. Jackbox's bounded custom-episode model makes this path feel more plausible than a full public UGC marketplace. [Custom episodes](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794763590167-Using-custom-or-user-created-episodes), [`REQUIREMENTS.md`](../../REQUIREMENTS.md)

## Key Tradeoffs And Hidden Assumptions

- [INFERRED] **Watchability versus solver purity.** Jackbox-style shared-screen ritual and audience affordances improve social energy, but too much emphasis here could weaken the expert-recognition challenge that makes Prix Guesser distinct. [`PROJECT.md`](../../PROJECT.md), [`CONVERGENCE.md`](../../audits/2026-04-08-pre-execution-review/CONVERGENCE.md)
- [INFERRED] **Wrapper breadth versus authored-content depth.** GeoGuessr and Kahoot both demonstrate wrapper expansion, but Prix Guesser's corpus is much more handcrafted; adding live, async, replay, and community surfaces too early could outpace content quality. [Daily Challenge](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017720198-What-is-the-Daily-Challenge), [Assign](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039411334-How-to-assign-a-kahoot-in-web-platform), [`PROJECT.md`](../../PROJECT.md)
- [INFERRED] **Low-friction guests versus durable identity.** Jackbox proves no-account entry can work well, while Kahoot shows the value of optional player identifiers for reports. Prix Guesser likely wants the former first and the latter only when calibration/history makes it necessary. [Accounts/subscriptions](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794759347735-Do-games-require-an-account-or-subscription-to-play), [Player identifier](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036178314)
- [INFERRED] **Local ritual versus hosted reliability.** Jackbox-like local simplicity is appealing, but the repo's planned room authority and reconnect work imply that Prix Guesser will eventually need more service-like guarantees than a packaged party game. [`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md), [Remote play](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794770038423-Can-I-play-Jackbox-Games-remotely-)

## Disconfirming Or Tensioning Evidence

- [CONFIRMED] GeoGuessr's official growth signals suggest that a geography product can become very large without centering authored reveals or sports-specific content. That weakens any claim that Prix Guesser must become more Jackbox-like to grow. [GeoGuessr career site](https://career.geoguessr.com/), [What is GeoGuessr?](https://geoguessr.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9486501024401-What-is-GeoGuessr)
- [CONFIRMED] Jackbox's remote-play guidance still relies heavily on Zoom/Discord/Twitch-style distribution, which may mean that "good enough" remote play can be much cheaper than building first-class browser-hosted rooms immediately. [Remote play](https://support.jackboxgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/15794770038423-Can-I-play-Jackbox-Games-remotely-)
- [CONFIRMED] Kahoot's support docs show participant-device question display, preview tooling, and optional player identifiers solving accessibility and operational issues that a host-screen-pure stance might miss. [Participant screens](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003197928-Kahoot-live-game-see-questions-on-participant-s-screen), [Preview](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/29968568026131-How-to-preview-a-kahoot), [Player identifier](https://support.kahoot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036178314)
- [INFERRED] Together, these counterexamples imply that Prix Guesser should be careful not to romanticize "sports-broadcast party game" so much that it ignores simpler, proven operational conveniences.

## What Could Change This View

- [INFERRED] If the user decides that public daily play, ladders, and larger-scale remote competition matter much sooner than currently documented, GeoGuessr becomes a much stronger master analogy and Jackbox becomes more local/ritual garnish than product spine.
- [INFERRED] If playtests show that the strongest fun comes from couch-night reveals and banter rather than precision solving, Jackbox's model becomes more central and the async/challenge path can stay secondary longer.
- [INFERRED] If authored content proves too expensive to maintain, Kahoot-style tooling, preview, and semi-structured author workflows become more urgent than richer spectator features.
- [HYPOTHESIS] If a credible community-support model appears that directly rewards high-quality pack authorship and hosting costs, the "trusted-circle curation" path may become materially more attractive than this pass can currently justify.
