## Research Instructions

Look for adjacent products or trajectories relevant to:

- browser-based party play with phones/controllers
- geography or clue-driven social deduction / recognition play
- async challenge structures and leaderboards
- private-to-public transition patterns
- streamer/spectator design considerations
- donation/community-supported indie products

The aim is not to prove Prix Guesser should mimic any one precedent.

The aim is to ask:

- what worked
- what constraints shaped those products
- what patterns seem reusable
- where Prix Guesser might intentionally diverge

Helpful prompts:

- Which reference products are genuinely analogous, and in what limited sense?
- Which products solved onboarding elegantly?
- Which products show a transition from small/private to larger/public operation?
- Where do analogies break down because Prix Guesser is more content-authored, more sport-specific, or more watchability-driven?
- Where is there whitespace for a more transparent, community-shaped, or hybrid local/hosted model?
