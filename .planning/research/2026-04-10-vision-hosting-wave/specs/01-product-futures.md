## Research Instructions

Do not reduce the product to a false binary like "local party" versus "solo async" unless the evidence demands it.

Instead:

- map multiple plausible stage-shapes
- describe how they can coexist or sequence over time
- identify what kind of "core" each future would imply in practice
- identify the user-experience consequences of centering one shape versus another
- examine whether "watchability" is one property or several different properties hiding under one word

Helpful prompts:

- What is the mature public-facing product space here?
- What is the likely first privately-tested shape?
- Which later wrappers feel like extensions of the same substrate versus different products entirely?
- What is the role of single-player in a product whose emotional fantasy is social?
- What patterns from adjacent products help distinguish "game mode", "wrapper", "session format", and "platform shell"?

## Output Emphasis

I want an exploratory map, not a recommendation memo.

Your findings should preserve ambiguity where appropriate, but still clarify the terrain.

Include:

- at least 3 viable long-arc product trajectories
- a section on `Kinds Of Watchability`
- a section on `What Must Be Protected Early If The Product Expands Later`
