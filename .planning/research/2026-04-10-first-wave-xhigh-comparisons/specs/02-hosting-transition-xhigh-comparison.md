## Research Instructions

Investigate whether a web-first product can plausibly support all of these stages without becoming incoherent or operationally reckless.

You should attend to:

- LAN feasibility and join patterns
- self-hosting options and tunnels
- what the real operational breakpoints are
- what more money actually buys
- what kinds of failure users feel first
- what forms of transparency preserve trust
- where capacity messaging, queueing, waitlisting, or staged access become honest vs manipulative
- whether browser-first, portable local-host packaging, and downloadable operator bundles can sensibly coexist

Do not flatten this into a vendor bakeoff.

Map the transition pathways, the real constraints, and the places where product shape and hosting strategy co-determine one another.
