# Lane G2 Task Spec

## What this is

This is a corrective rerun of Lane G.

It exists because Lane G found a stronger territory, but still failed in two important ways:

- it locked too quickly onto a few "respectable" realizations instead of genuinely roaming the absurd design space
- it underpitched the ideas, leaving them too brief to evaluate properly

This rerun is not meant to replace Lane G entirely. It is meant to do the missing work:

- explore the full weirdness range
- include, among other possibilities, spaces where the control room runs on childish, ritualistic, contradictory, overengineered, or otherwise absurd logic
- produce fuller pitches rather than thin labels

## Core correction

The previous lane still leaned too much toward plausible analogies and too little toward the broader absurd space, including possibilities like:

- bizarre interface logic
- badly matched tools
- Ferrari-brain support chaos
- systems that are funny because they are structurally deranged, not just slightly overloaded

Examples of the kind of terrain this rerun must take seriously:

- a pit wall monitor that is basically a baby toy and only yields info when shapes are matched correctly
- support systems that use obviously wrong affordances for high-stakes decisions
- contradictory control surfaces that feel like an institution operating on alien logic
- literal pit-wall grammar dropped into ridiculous scenarios without losing F1 voltage

The output should not merely say these are interesting. It should pitch them.

Important clarification:

- the examples in this spec and in creator feedback are not a taxonomy
- they are probes into the design space
- the lane must not overfit to them as if the job were to instantiate each example once
- these examples are not mandatory branches; they are prompts that indicate how open the search should remain

The real task is to explore the broader terrain they point toward.

## Creator steering

Use these as direct constraints:

- do not collapse onto only plausible or semi-realistic variants
- do not assume absurdity is secondary or only garnish
- do not assume Ferrari-chaos means only overloaded-but-still-sane strategy screens
- explore the possibility that the funniest and strongest control-room branches operate on warped or alien support logic
- retain the possibility of literal F1, adjacent analogy, and full absurdity as coequal branches
- give enough pitch detail that the creator can actually imagine the game
- treat creator-supplied examples as sparks, not boxes to fill
- search for additional weird logics beyond the examples already named

## Anti-goals

Do NOT:

- produce only another shortlist of labels
- produce only realistic strategy variants
- reduce absurdity to reskinning
- give one-sentence "what if" prompts without loop, social shape, and replay implications
- quietly rank plausible ideas above bizarre ones just because they are easier to explain

## Required lenses

### 1. Strange support-system possibilities

Explore a broad range of control-room designs, including cases where the support system is funny because it is fundamentally wrong for the task:

- baby-toy interfaces
- ritualized nonsense
- childish shape-sorting
- contradictory dashboards
- perverse UX
- pseudo-helpful bureaucracy
- random-seeming but secretly gameable signal systems

Ask when useful:

- what is the player actually doing?
- what information or action does the interface or ritual gate?
- how does the wrongness, excess, mismatch, or confusion itself become play?

Do not stop at the named examples, but also do not treat this cluster as mandatory.

Keep searching for additional absurd operating logics such as:

- superstitious procedures
- ceremonial approvals
- family-game / toy logic
- fake sophistication
- nationalist / institutional / team-culture parody
- impossible overinstrumentation
- charmingly useless helper systems
- mutually incompatible expert tools

### 2. Full weirdness range

Do not stop at:

- literal F1
- adjacent plausible analogy

Also allow:

- total comedic transposition
- civic / domestic / delivery / kitchen / office / ritual contexts
- situations where a literal pit wall is absurdly overapplied

But do not assume these are the only contextual directions, or that every strong idea must become highly absurd.

The lane should also ask:

- what other scenario spaces become funny once pit-wall grammar is overapplied?
- what different kinds of absurdity preserve different kinds of F1-rootedness?

### 3. Pitch depth

For each strong idea, provide enough detail to answer:

- what it is
- what players actually do
- why it is funny / vivid
- why it could still be a real game
- where replay comes from
- what the best local and online forms are

### 4. F1-rootedness without realism worship

For each idea, say what kind of F1-rootedness it preserves:

- radio language
- push/box rhythm
- traffic / line / grip logic
- pit-stop / support hierarchy
- meme voltage
- strategy panic
- rivalry structure
- institutional dysfunction

### 5. Distinction from prior control-room ideas

Make clear whether an idea is:

- `Pit Wall`
- `Driver Override`
- `Bad Wall`
- `Wall of Noise`

or something genuinely new.

## Quality bar

Good output for this rerun should:

- visibly go weirder than Lane G
- still preserve real game shape
- include ideas beyond narrow realism logic without forcing all strong ideas into one style of absurdity
- produce fuller pitches, not just names
- give the creator enough material to say "this one feels alive / dead / funny / too thin"
- show evidence of genuine exploration beyond the prompt examples rather than merely organizing those examples
