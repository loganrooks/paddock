# Lane F Task Spec

## What this lane is

This is a supplemental exploratory lane opened after the first net-new-family pass.

Its focus is not generic new families. Its focus is `driver-themed game possibilities`.

That means:

- current / recent drivers
- greats where useful
- personality quirks
- crash reputations
- media personas
- cultural fluency
- the question of when a driver motif becomes an actual mechanic instead of just flavor

This lane should treat the current seeded driver ideas as launch points, not boundaries.

## Why this lane exists

The current corpus already contains several strong driver-inflected seeds:

- `The Verstappen Game`
- `Talibantonelli / Al Mer Qaedes / Osama bin Russell`
- Hamilton-inflected fashion directions inside `Paddock Fashion`
- broader notes on driver personality as mechanic in `IDEAS-cross-cutting.md`

The creator wants another exploratory pass that asks:

- what other driver-themed ideas are genuinely strong?
- which driver quirks map to mechanics rather than just prompt flavor?
- which ideas could become real couch-first or online/social games?
- which realizations cash cultural fluency without collapsing into generic trivia or generic improv?

## Creator steering for this lane

Use these as direct constraints:

- the creator explicitly wants a driver-themed ideation pass after reviewing Lane E
- the strongest seeded examples are the user-origin driver-inflected ideas:
  `Verstappen`, `Hamilton` fashion direction, and `Talibantonelli / Russell` chaos
- this lane should explore current / recent grid drivers and greats where useful
- the point is to cash cultural currency and driver quirks, not just slap driver names on generic party mechanics
- some driver-themed directions may be:
  - separate games
  - unlockable modes within a game
  - alternative match types
  - driver packs / themed content
- couch-first value matters, but online play, streamer readability, and community potential also matter
- unlock ladders, selectable vehicle classes, level packs, and replay structures are relevant where they fit the game rather than being forced everywhere

## Anti-goals

Do NOT:

- reduce driver-themed ideation to trivia or quote recognition
- force every driver quirk into the same ontology
- assume every good driver idea should become a standalone game
- assume every good driver idea should become only a cosmetic skin
- default to off-screen talking/performance as the mechanic
- pitch generic party-game templates with driver names pasted on top
- optimize for meme surface while ignoring replay and return
- ignore tone, taste, and public-surface risk where a joke depends on private-group context

## Required lenses

### 1. Driver-to-mechanic lens

For each promising driver direction, ask:

- is the driver mostly flavor, or is there a mechanic here?
- what behavior, reputation, or social script is doing the real work?
- does the mechanic stand on its own even if the player only half-knows the lore?

### 2. Design-terrain lens

For each promising seeded or new idea, ask:

- what is the core fantasy?
- what are the strongest competing realizations?
- which realizations feel stronger for couch?
- which look stronger for online sync?
- which open community or streamer possibilities?
- which realizations cheapen or flatten the idea?

### 3. Native-ontology lens

Do not force one structure on every idea.

Instead ask:

- what are the real moving parts of this game?
- where do distinctions like `mode`, `match type`, `unlock`, `driver roster`, `vehicle class`, `hazard profile`, `role`, or `content pack` actually belong?
- what ontology seems native to this game rather than imposed from outside?

### 4. Cultural-fluency lens

Ask:

- what knowledge level does the joke or mechanic require?
- what still works for mixed-knowledge groups?
- what becomes richer for hardcore fans without excluding everyone else?
- which drivers or eras carry the strongest shared public signal?

### 5. Replay / return lens

Ask:

- what makes the idea instantly funny or vivid?
- what makes the same group want another round?
- what could support unlocks, rotations, packs, ladders, or friend-group memory?

### 6. Context / community lens

For every strong direction, say something about:

- couch / host-screen value
- private online sync value
- streamer / spectator readability
- community or persistent-group possibilities
- whether the best form is context-specialist or context-flexible

### 7. Overlap / integration lens

Ask:

- does this want to be its own game?
- should it live as a mode or branch inside an existing game?
- is it best as a driver-themed content pack for another mechanic family?
- does it suggest a whole driver-themed product cluster or night structure?
