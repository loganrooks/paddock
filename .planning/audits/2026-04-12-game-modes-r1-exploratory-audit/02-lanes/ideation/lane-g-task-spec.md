# Lane G Task Spec

## What this lane is

This is a focused lane to run before Round 2 proper.

It exists because the recent conversation surfaced two linked concerns that should not be left as scattered comments:

1. Some of the strongest ideas in the project need to hold both:
   - F1-specific meme / cultural charge
   - legitimate game structure and replay

2. There is a promising asymmetric `driver / pit wall / control room` territory that may want:
   - online play
   - unconventional local play
   - host-screen + private devices
   - phone / separate-room / different-room arrangements
   - comedic and confusing interfaces
   - perhaps Ferrari-coded chaos, but not necessarily locked to Ferrari

This lane should not treat those as narrow footnotes. They are design-terrain questions worth a dedicated pass.

## Why this should happen before Round 2

If this is left to Round 2 broadly, it will get diluted into larger portfolio discussion.

This lane should sharpen:

- where cultural specificity is load-bearing
- where abstraction helps versus kills the idea
- what replay / return structure can sit underneath strong meme surfaces
- what the strongest realizations of asymmetric driver/control-room play actually are

That will make Round 2 cleaner instead of noisier.

## Creator steering

Use these as direct constraints:

- do not over-purify meme-heavy games into clean abstractions that lose their F1-cultural electricity
- do not keep only the meme either; search for the replay/return structure underneath
- the `Verstappen` and `Talibantonelli / Russell` territory should be read through that tension explicitly
- the `driver / pit wall / control room` territory should be reopened as something broader than sober strategy
- Ferrari-coded chaos is one live direction, but not the only one
- creative local play counts:
  same house, different rooms, phones, hidden screens, improvised call setups, `Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes`-style arrangements
- do not assume this territory must be couch-only, online-only, or strictly literal-racing-only
- do not treat literal F1, adjacent analogy, and full absurdity as a hierarchy
- allow realizations that remain strongly F1-rooted through grammar, social script, or meme logic even when the scenario itself is absurd

## Anti-goals

Do NOT:

- reduce this lane to "should Ferrari be a theme?"
- reduce it to only one game pitch
- assume that if something is meme-heavy it cannot have real design depth
- assume that if something has a legitimate loop it should be cleaned of its specific cultural source
- collapse all asymmetric control-room ideas into the existing `Pit Wall` framing
- ignore comedic interface design, bad comms, contradiction, or misdesigned control surfaces as design material

## Required lenses

### 1. Cultural charge vs mechanical spine

For the strongest meme-heavy ideas, ask:

- what is the culturally specific thing doing the work?
- what happens if it is abstracted away?
- what replay systems can sit underneath without flattening it?
- what is actually load-bearing:
  naming, audio, presentation, mythology, social script, or something else?

### 2. Asymmetric control-room terrain

Explore driver / wall / control-room play across multiple realizations:

- sober authority conflict
- chaotic bad-comms comedy
- overloaded dashboard/interface satire
- literal racing scenarios
- stranger abstracted scenarios that preserve the same asymmetry
- absurd scenarios with a literal pit wall dropped in

Do not rank these as "close to F1" versus "far from F1" too early.

Instead ask:

- what kind of F1-rootedness is being preserved here?
- is it preserved through control-room structure, audio language, timing pressure, racing-line logic, push/box rhythm, rivalry grammar, meme charge, or something else?

### 3. Context experimentation

Do not limit this to standard couch or standard online.

Explicitly test:

- online private sync
- host-screen + phones
- local but separated players
- same house / different rooms / phone calls
- spectator and streamer roles

### 4. Distinction from existing ideas

Ask:

- when is this just `Pit Wall` again?
- when does it become a clearly different game or branch?
- is the distinction coming from interface chaos, driver authority, comms discipline, tone, or something else?

### 5. Return structure

For every strong direction, ask:

- what gets people in the door?
- what gets them to run it again?
- what could support packs, ladders, scenarios, unlocks, or recurring-group memory?
