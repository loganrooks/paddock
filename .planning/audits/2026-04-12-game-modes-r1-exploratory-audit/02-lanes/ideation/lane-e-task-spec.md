# Lane E Task Spec

## What this lane is

This is a supplemental exploratory lane opened after Round 1.

It is not a loose “more ideas please” dump. It is a `portfolio-gap / net-new families` lane.

Your job is to use the current Round 1 audit results as a map of what is already strong, what is under-shaped, and what kinds of fun are still missing or underrepresented. Then you should propose:

- new adjacent variants within existing families
- overlapping ideas that are clearly stronger than current versions
- genuinely net-new game families
- themed party / playlist / curation structures that group modes together in interesting ways

## Why this lane exists

Round 1 clarified several strong existing families:

- `Words of Wisdom`
- `The Stewards' Room`
- `Paddock Fashion`
- predator-hunter action (`The Verstappen Game` / `Talibantonelli / Osama bin Russell`)

It also identified several under-shaped but promising branches:

- `commentary / crowd recognition`
- `Team Principal's Desk`
- `The Grid Walk`
- `Meme Prompts`
- `Pit Stop Co-op`

This lane exists because the creator wants to:

- see more options across multiple categories, not just one
- get more couch-first possibilities because that is the near-term target
- keep online / streamer / virality potential in view for the longer term
- allow overlap with existing ideas when a new version is clearly better
- explore whether themed parties / curated mode groupings could become part of the product shape

## Creator steering for this lane

Use these as direct constraints:

- the creator wants a little bit of everything:
  headliners, short-session spikes, hidden discoveries, and broader idea-map coverage
- more couch/host-screen possibilities are especially valuable because that is the near-term target
- online gameplay still matters because long-term virality / streamer play / shared online play matter
- sabotage and deception are not absent from the corpus, but more ideas in that territory are welcome
- themed party curation / grouped playlists of modes is itself worth exploring
- the lane is allowed to produce ideas that overlap existing ones if they are clearly better reframings
- the creator wants a broader field, not just a tiny shortlist

## Anti-goals

Do NOT:
- just restate current modes with tiny cosmetic changes
- only pitch serious modes or only pitch meme modes
- only pitch couch ideas or only pitch online ideas
- default to one ideal audience or one ideal social context
- collapse to feasibility-first reasoning
- treat “more ideas” as permission to produce generic party-game sludge
- overreward novelty while ignoring replay, return loops, or group memory
- ignore the geography-anchor product reality and drift into a disconnected anything-goes party-game app

## Required lenses

### 1. Portfolio-gap lens

Ask:
- what kinds of fun are still underrepresented?
- what mode roles are missing:
  headliner, opener, palate cleanser, hidden-discovery oddity, remote specialist, recurring-group staple
- what contexts are still under-served?
- what tonal ranges are missing or thin?

### 2. Adjacent-expansion lens

Ask:
- where can an existing strong family be extended with a materially new branch?
- where can an under-shaped branch be rescued with a better framing?
- where does a current idea want a more constrained or more dramatic version?

### 3. Overlap / replacement lens

Ask:
- where is there a clearly better version of an existing idea?
- where can a current thin idea be replaced by a stronger family built on the same core fantasy?

### 4. Net-new family lens

Ask:
- what promising family is absent from the current portfolio?
- what would make it distinctly F1-inflected rather than generic with stickers?
- what would make it replayable, not just clever on paper?

### 5. Context-profile lens

For every strong idea or family, say:
- couch / host-screen strength
- private online sync strength
- spectator / streamer strength
- async or appointment value where relevant
- whether the mode is context-specialist or context-flexible

### 6. Virality vs return-loop lens

For every strong idea or family, say:
- what makes it instantly legible, dramatic, or clip-worthy
- what makes the same group want another round, another night, or another pack

### 7. Themed-party / curation lens

Ask:
- can modes be grouped into deliberately curated party formats?
- are there night structures built around:
  chaos, courtroom, radio/cultural fluency, fashion/showmanship, sabotage, race-weekend drama, etc.?
- can themed bundles make weaker modes more compelling as part of a sequence rather than alone?

## Quality bar

Good output for this lane should:

- produce a broad field without collapsing into generic sludge
- distinguish adjacent branch ideas from genuinely net-new families
- notice where a stronger overlap should replace a weaker existing framing
- respect couch-first near-term priorities without losing long-term online imagination
- produce ideas that have both `hook` and `return-loop` logic
- suggest themed curation structures that actually change how the portfolio could be experienced
- surface a few strong candidates that feel like they could become real load-bearing branches, not just a fun brainstorming pile
