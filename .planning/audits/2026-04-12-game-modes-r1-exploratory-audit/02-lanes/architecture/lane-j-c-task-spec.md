# Lane J-C Task Spec

## Focus

Research hidden-info, asymmetric comms, and private-room topology systems with emphasis on engineering exposure.

Prioritize:

- technical talks or postmortems
- official docs exposing topology, moderation, visibility, or room assumptions
- engineering-adjacent docs where direct engineering material is scarce

## Reference territory

Possible ecosystems include, but are not limited to:

- Among Us
- Jackbox deception / private-phone games
- Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes
- Artemis
- Blood on the Clocktower digital / moderation materials
- Spaceteam
- other asymmetric comms or hidden-info systems with useful engineering exposure

## Questions

- What concrete topology/visibility/moderation problems are exposed?
- What mechanisms are used for secrecy, role-specific views, structured reveals, moderation, or voice/comms assumptions?
- Where do the sources expose engineering, and where do they only expose product constraints?
- What tradeoffs are visible?
- Which parts seem relevant to Pit Wall / Bad Wall / Driver Override / sabotage or rumor branches?
