# Lane J-B Task Spec

## Focus

Research real-time action, racing, and larger-room systems with emphasis on engineering exposure.

Prioritize:

- engineering blogs
- engine docs
- technical presentations
- postmortems
- official support or sporting-code docs as secondary evidence for actual field limits, splits, or topology

## Reference territory

Possible ecosystems include, but are not limited to:

- Fortnite / Unreal replication material
- Fall Guys
- Battlefield
- Trackmania
- Rocket Racing
- iRacing
- other racing or large-action systems that expose concrete engineering

## Questions

- What concrete technical problems are actually discussed?
- What mechanisms are used:
  interest management, sharding, heats, splits, relevance filtering, ghosting, collision reduction, authority models, etc.?
- What player-count claims are actually backed by engineering exposure?
- What is direct evidence versus inference from product shape?
- What tradeoffs do these systems appear to accept?
- Which aspects seem relevant to any plausible future F1 action modes here?
