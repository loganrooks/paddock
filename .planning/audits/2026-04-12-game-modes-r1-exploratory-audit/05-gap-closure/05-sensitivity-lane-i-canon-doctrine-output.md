## 5. Bounded-open anti-hard-coding findings

`[evidenced:cited:internal]` The most important bounded-open risk is that canon example lists overname some branches and undername others:

- `PROJECT.md` names `live room, solo practice, frozen challenge, or later spectator-facing shell` as the session-wrapper vocabulary, and its critical open question names `solo practice`, `frozen challenge links`, and `spectator-facing shells` as the main later-wrapper options (`PROJECT.md:45-53`, `PROJECT.md:175-180`). A1 and E1 say the actual live bounded-open picture is wider:
  - the low-burden contest is still mostly `showcase aftermath` versus `share-by-link / challenge`
  - `bounded live audience` is real but heavier
  - `hosted-night recap / session report` is currently the strongest first explicit shell
  - `featured challenge result` remains a real rival (`05-sensitivity-docket-a1-low-burden-wrapper-ordering-output.md:175-216`, `05-sensitivity-docket-e1-first-explicit-shell-output.md:170-191`)
- `LONG-ARC.md` keeps later wrappers alive, but its named examples are still `async challenge`, `solo support shell`, and `spectator or streamer-adjacent shell` (`LONG-ARC.md:39-45`). That is safe only if read as illustrative. A1 and E1 both warn that repeated `challenge`/`spectator` naming can quietly erase low-burden aftermath or recap/report shells by omission (`05-sensitivity-docket-a1-low-burden-wrapper-ordering-output.md:107-120`, `05-sensitivity-docket-e1-first-explicit-shell-output.md:121-134`).
- `REQUIREMENTS.md` is the most fragile illustration surface:
  - `WRAP-01` explicitly names `async or solo challenge`
  - `RET-01` names `daily or recurring challenge`
  - `RET-04` names `replay or structured review`
  - `DEF-01` names `share-by-link flows`
  - `SEAM-05` names `unlisted challenges` and later spectator/public read surfaces (`REQUIREMENTS.md:89-102`, `REQUIREMENTS.md:131-143`)
  Those are all individually defensible, but together they can make `challenge` look like the chosen reuse surface, the chosen cadence winner, and the chosen explicit-shell residue unless later readers keep the A1/E1/E2 distinctions active (`05-sensitivity-docket-a1-low-burden-wrapper-ordering-output.md:140-156`, `05-sensitivity-docket-e1-first-explicit-shell-output.md:177-205`, `05-sensitivity-docket-e2-event-memory-cadence-mastery-output.md:151-188`).

`[assumed:reasoned:internal]` The anti-hard-coding burden here is interpretive rather than immediate-edit mandatory:

- `PROJECT.md` should continue to treat its later-wrapper examples as possibility space, not as a shortlist with aftermath already removed.
- `LONG-ARC.md` should continue to treat its wrapper list as examples, not as a ranked or exhaustive taxonomy.
- `REQUIREMENTS.md` should continue to treat future capabilities as illustrative seams, not as a canonical ordering of post-private product surfaces.
