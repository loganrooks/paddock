- `REQUIREMENTS.md` was the highest-risk quiet hard-coding surface in the manifest, especially around `WRAP-01`, `RET-01`, `RET-04`, `SEAM-04` through `SEAM-08`, and `DEF-01` through `DEF-05`.
- `LONG-ARC.md` was the right place to make farther doctrine explicit so Milestone 2 and later work do not depend on audit memory for wrapper, host, money, discovery, and memory distinctions.
- `PROJECT.md` needed a lighter but real rebalance because the compact story was still over-naming challenge and spectator futures relative to aftermath/recap and host/discovery seams.
- `ROADMAP.md` needed carry-forward constraints that preserve open families without widening Milestone 01 execution scope or changing phase order.
- Governance docs were reviewed after patching to check for contradiction or duplication pressure; no governance edit was justified because those files still point to the canon surfaces correctly and do not conflict with the uplifted doctrine.
