# 05 Remediation Packet D: Curated Contribution And Discovery Posture

## Gap justification

This packet exists to address:

- `GCO-00`
- `GCO-00A`
- `GCO-06`
- `GCO-08C`
- `GCO-09`
- `GCO-09A`
- `GCO-09B`

Why these belong together:

- the unresolved issue is not just “should community contribute?”
- it is how content supply, curation, endorsement, activation, and discovery posture interact without forcing an open-platform model

This packet is not trying to close:

- a public creator marketplace
- fully open publishing
- the entire content-flywheel or recurrence question by itself

## Purpose

Reopen the middle space between internal-only authorship and open publishing and determine what curated contribution posture, if any, is worth preserving.

## Focus

- curated partner authors
- reviewed submissions
- directory-listed but unsupported additions
- endorsement / activation / removal posture
- what discovery promises
- how this changes long-term content supply assumptions
