# 05 Gap Closure Verification R2 B

## Standard correction applied

For this rerun I counted:

- `direct external grounding` only where this round itself surfaces outside sources, chiefly `05-gap-closure-reference-patterns-output.md:97-235`
- `transitive support` where the bundle cites older repo-local research that itself has real external methods and source ledgers, such as `02-funding-access-and-transparency-models.md:17-25,312-396`, `05-xhigh-comparison-cooperative-scaling-and-p2p-feasibility.md:16-20,191-258`, and `02-hosting-transition.md:27-41,203-263`
- `internal doctrine reasoning` only where the claim is truly about local product ordering or deferral rather than real-world plausibility

That correction matters because this bundle is not empty circularity, but most of its direct external grounding is still concentrated in one memo rather than distributed into the lanes that use it.

## Bundle-wide verdict under the corrected standard

`Patch-first plus one required narrow follow-up research lane.`

Under the corrected standard, the bundle is not closure-grade end-to-end:

- Lanes `A` and `B` are still acceptable as mostly internal doctrine-ordering work.
- Lanes `C` and `D` do not need brand-new research, but they do need direct integration of the bundle's existing external memo before they should influence sensitivity or canon work.
- Lane `E` splits in two:
  - `enough to proceed`: keep one canonical self-hostable authoritative bundle alive, keep trusted operator-hosted authoritative rooms alive, and keep full browser P2P out of the main transition ladder
  - `not externally closed yet`: ranking `sanctioned mirrors / small host collectives` against `official hosted convenience`
- The bundle still lacks the actual post-lane converged synthesis. `05-gap-closure-synthesis.md` is still the topology setter that describes the later synthesis as future work (`05-gap-closure-synthesis.md:114-214`).

So the next sensitivity pass should not treat the current bundle as if all five lanes are equally research-closed.

## Where the first verification pass was too lenient or too strict

### Too lenient

1. `Lane E follow-up was treated as optional.`
   The earlier verifier correctly noticed that the strongest unresolved pressure was the `sanctioned mirrors / official hosted convenience` branch, but under the corrected standard that is no longer an optional extra. If that branch is going to influence sensitivity or canon work, it needs direct follow-up research first. The current memo explicitly leaves that question open (`05-gap-closure-reference-patterns-output.md:195-199,223-235`), and Lane E itself lists it as unresolved (`05-gap-closure-lane-e-grassroots-transition-hosting-output.md:178-185`).

2. `The existence of the memo was treated as more closure than its actual distribution justified.`
   Lanes `C` and `E` still say the memo is missing (`05-gap-closure-lane-c-community-shells-output.md:38,83`; `05-gap-closure-lane-e-grassroots-transition-hosting-output.md:73,272,295-297`). Under the corrected standard, a memo that exists but is not actually integrated is not enough to call those claims externally grounded.

### Too strict, if read broadly

1. `Lane D does not need a brand-new research lane just to keep its doctrine alive.`
   The bundle already has direct external support for the `support -> hosted convenience -> stronger obligation` distinction through Nabu Casa and Foundry (`05-gap-closure-reference-patterns-output.md:143-167`), plus older source-backed transitive support from the April 10 funding work (`02-funding-access-and-transparency-models.md:17-25,69-120`). That is enough for a doctrine-level ladder once the memo is actually integrated.

2. `Lane C does not need new research to keep the shell ladder alive.`
   The existing memo already supplies the missing audience-rights and bounded-public pattern family through Jackbox and Discord Stage Channels (`05-gap-closure-reference-patterns-output.md:97-119`). The problem is memo distribution and stale provenance, not a missing research domain.

## Follow-up research required items

### 1. Trusted volunteer hosts vs sanctioned mirrors vs official hosted convenience

This is the one domain that should be upgraded from earlier `patch-first` language to `follow-up research required`.

The current bundle can already justify:

- `trusted operator-hosted authoritative rooms` as a real transition seam
- `full browser P2P` as the wrong main branch

It cannot yet justify, with enough direct grounding for sensitivity or canon work:

- whether `sanctioned mirrors / small host collectives` should be treated as a serious near-future branch rather than a merely possible one
- whether `official hosted convenience` should outrank those collectives once use expands beyond a trusted technical circle
- what minimum standards a promoted community host would actually need

The missing research shape is narrow and comparative:

- real examples of promoted volunteer/community hosts or trusted third-party operators
- explicit minimum standards around backups, emergency access, shutdown notice, version/pack compatibility, operator runbooks, and host labeling
- clearer comparisons between `one official hosted path` and `a small curated host ecology`

Why this is required now:

- the memo itself says the references do not settle whether sanctioned mirrors or host collectives are worth the burden before simpler hosted convenience (`05-gap-closure-reference-patterns-output.md:197-199,226-235`)
- Lane E's current ranking still leans mostly on internal and transitive support rather than direct external comparison (`05-gap-closure-lane-e-grassroots-transition-hosting-output.md:148-185`)

### 2. No broader new external lane is required right now

The rerun does **not** justify:

- a broad business-plan research pass
- a pricing research lane
- a public-scale systems lane
- a new shell-ordering research lane for Lane C
- a new monetization research lane for Lane D

Those remain either deferred or already supported enough for doctrine-level carry-forward once the patch-first work is done.

## What can proceed anyway

The following can still influence the next stage, with the stated limits:

- `Lane A` as doctrine-level content-flywheel direction
- `Lane B` as doctrine-level recurrence ordering
- `Lane C` after memo integration, as shell-ordering direction rather than as a claim that market plausibility is now fully settled
- `Lane D` after memo integration, as a doctrine-level `support-first, access-separate` ladder
- `Lane E` only on:
  - preserve one canonical self-hostable authoritative bundle
  - keep trusted volunteer authoritative hosts alive as the primary grassroots seam
  - keep full browser P2P outside the main transition ladder

## Blocking issues before the next sensitivity pass

1. `No converged post-lane synthesis exists yet.`
   That is a hard structural blocker even apart from research quality.

2. `The current Lane E host-ranking cannot be treated as closed.`
   If the next sensitivity pass is supposed to test the full bundle as closure-complete, it should be blocked until the narrow host-topology follow-up research is done.

3. `A narrower sensitivity pass could proceed only if it is explicitly reframed.`
   That reframing would need to say:
   - Lane E is closed only on `trusted operator-hosted authoritative rooms` versus `browser P2P`
   - the `sanctioned mirrors / official hosted convenience` comparison remains open
   - canon or roadmap language must not silently ratify that unresolved ranking

## Recommended next research topology

1. `Patch provenance and memo distribution first.`
   Fix Lane C, D, and E so the bundle's existing direct external support is actually visible where those lanes depend on it.

2. `Run one narrow comparative host-topology research lane.`
   Focus only on promoted volunteer hosts, sanctioned mirrors / small collectives, official hosted convenience, and the standards that separate them.

3. `Write the actual converged synthesis.`
   That synthesis should say plainly which parts of the bundle are:
   - internal doctrine
   - directly externally grounded in this round
   - only transitively supported
   - still open pending the host-topology follow-up lane

4. `Then run sensitivity.`
   If the follow-up lane is skipped, the sensitivity pass must be explicitly limited so it does not treat the unresolved Lane E branch as settled closure.

## Bottom line

The first verification pass was directionally right that the bundle was not ready as-is, but it was still too permissive about Lane E.

Under the corrected standard:

- `C` and `D` stay `patch-first`
- `A` and `B` can proceed as doctrine-level inputs
- `E` must be split:
  - `patch-first` for provenance and memo integration
  - `follow-up research required` for any claim that ranks `sanctioned mirrors / collectives` against `official hosted convenience`

That is the main upgrade from the earlier verifier judgment.
