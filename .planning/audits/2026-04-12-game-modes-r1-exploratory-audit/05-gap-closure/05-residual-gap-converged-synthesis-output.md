# 05 Residual Gap Converged Synthesis Output

## Synthesis framing

- Mode: `synthesis`
- Classification: `initial architecture research/planning`
- Gate verdict: `proceed to the distributed sensitivity pass only as a bounded multi-lane pass`
- The remediation synthesis already converted the packet bundle into a usable doctrine map, but its readiness verdict was still too coarse. The deferral/rejection review corrected semantic drift around `deferred`, `rejected-for-now`, `unresolved ranking`, and `still not sensitivity-ready`. The residual chunks then converted several vague residuals into one of four tighter forms:
  - a bounded ordering or ranking question
  - a preserve-only later seam
  - a reversal-sensitive rejected-for-now proposition
  - a narrower inquiry debt surface
- The residual round therefore did not fully close the mature-product answer. It made the gate sharper. The later distributed sensitivity pass is now justified only if it keeps five lanes separate:
  - `direct doctrine protection`
  - `bounded-open anti-hard-coding`
  - `preserve-only seam tracing`
  - `reversal-sensitive guardrails`
  - `inquiry-debt non-resolution`
- This artifact should replace any looser reading that the bundle is simply "ready" or "not ready." It is ready only in the constrained sense above.

## What the residual round tightened

- `Wrapper topology` is tighter. Chunk A confirmed that the wrapper family map from the remediation synthesis basically holds. The important residual change is that `first audience bundle` is now better understood as a staged ordering question inside `bounded live audience`, not as a missing peer family, and `daily/programmed challenge` is better translated as an editorial/programmed cadence layer rather than a peer wrapper.
- `Money-family discrimination` is tighter. Chunk B made the later money space less compressible: `editorial/content`, `hosted convenience`, and `paid guaranteed participation` are no longer one blurry `premium` ladder. `Editorial/content` now reads as the stronger general later-value family, `hosted convenience` as a more conditional service-bearing family, and `paid guaranteed participation` as outside the ordinary ladder entirely.
- `Hosting identity` is tighter. Chunk C sharpened `official hosted convenience` versus `sanctioned mirrors / collectives` into different later identities rather than one clean host ladder: the former is a `service identity` branch, the latter a `governance identity` branch. It also made `official hosted convenience + unpromoted private operators` legible as a real hybrid later shape.
- `Contribution/discovery identity` is tighter. Chunk D narrowed the strongest later middle-space answer toward `guest editor / editorial activation inside a Prix-curated library`, pushed `partner brand / creator profile` into a later, heavier, challengeable branch, and strengthened the placement of `directory-listed but unsupported` into advanced/admin/self-host surfaces rather than the core guest-facing library.
- `Recurrence and memory` are tighter. Chunk E converted `event memory` from a vague UI problem into an `event object threshold`, split `cadence` into distinct families, closed the `mastery surface family` more tightly without choosing a metric, and clarified that `group memory` can stay real while remaining shallower than player history or event memory.

## What remains open but bounded

- `Wrapper ordering`: the live wrapper question is now mostly the low-burden order between `showcase aftermath` and `share-by-link / challenge`, plus when `bounded live audience` becomes worth promoting for the bounded-event archetype. This is not a missing-family problem anymore.
- `First audience bundle`: the first bounded-audience bundle remains open between passive watchability and light interaction, but it is now bounded inside one family rather than floating as a separate architecture gap.
- `Later money-family ranking`: `editorial/content` is now the stronger general later default, but not a commitment. `Hosted convenience` remains materially live, especially for a remote-heavy future. The open question is no longer `what is premium?`; it is `when does convenience outrank content, if ever?`
- `Official hosting priority`: `official hosted convenience` is now more strongly preserved than `sanctioned mirrors / collectives`, but it is still not a chosen later winner. The open question is whether it remains a protected later branch or becomes an active priority once remote-private pain is real.
- `Guest-editor identity`: `guest editor / editorial activation` is now the strongest later default for approved outside packs, but it is still challengeable. The exact namespace, degree of co-branding, and any future graduation into stronger partner identity remain open.
- `Supporter-benefit shape`: exact low-obligation supporter benefits remain open because they still act as branch selectors. The residual work bounded the risk but did not close the perk set.
- `Mastery and cadence parameters`: the first mastery read, achievement-overlay visibility, and exact cadence labels remain open parameters, but they now sit inside tighter family distinctions.
- `First event shell worth persistence`: the residual round bounded the question to `which real event shell, if any, first crosses the event-object threshold`, but it did not decide that shell.

## What remains preserve-only

- `Ambient community later shape` remains a distinct later, discoverable, moderation-bearing service branch. Sensitivity should preserve the seam and prevent accidental foreclosure, not treat it as a near-term commitment.
- `Sanctioned mirrors / collectives` remain a live plural-host seam, but only as a preserve-only branch unless the product deliberately chooses a governed multi-host identity.
- `Mixed spectator-participant` audience forms remain preserve-only. Chunk A strengthened the passive-to-interactive audience ladder, but stronger audience submission/speaking forms still should not be pulled forward as assumed first-bundle doctrine.
- `Reviewed external submissions` remain a later conditional intake seam, not an active product direction.
- `Directory-listed but unsupported additions` remain preserve-only and should be preserved only in advanced/admin/self-host surfaces, not in the main guest-facing library.
- `Partner brand / creator profile discovery` remains preserve-only. It stays alive as a later creator-forward branch, but the residual round did not justify treating it as the default later identity.
- `Later event-object memory` remains preserve-only in branch terms. The seam must stay available, but sensitivity should not treat a concrete event-memory product layer as already owed.

## What remains reversal-sensitive

- `Open marketplace / public publishing` remains rejected-for-now. Sensitivity should only trace it where a reversal would materially change authority, moderation, discovery, and support posture.
- `Paid guaranteed participation / priority access` remains rejected-for-now as an ordinary later option. Any re-entry here is a move into booking/service semantics, not a harmless perk decision.
- `Official hosted convenience as the first-value or near-term default branch` remains rejected-for-now even though later official hosting remains live.
- `Promoted sanctioned mirrors / collectives as the obvious next rung` remain rejected-for-now even though plural-host governance remains preserved as a seam.
- `Support-plus-beta` as a default `membership` or normal later money family remains rejected-for-now. Only a narrow bounded test lane remains conditionally alive.

## What remains inquiry debt

- `Concrete event-memory / UI commitment` remains inquiry debt. Chunk E tightened the threshold for when event memory becomes real, but it did not close the first explicit event-memory surface, navigation model, or retention depth.
- `First explicit event shell` remains inquiry debt in concrete product form. The residual round bounded the candidate space, but it did not decide whether the first eventized surface is a hosted-night recap, a featured challenge result, a weekend board, or something else.
- `Exact event-memory visibility and permanence` remain inquiry debt. The bundle is now ready to protect the seam, not to design the concrete board, history depth, or exposure rules.

## What the distributed sensitivity pass must test

- Whether canon and phase artifacts preserve the wrapper-family split and avoid silently naming one universal first-wrapper answer.
- Whether current language keeps `first audience bundle` inside the bounded-audience ladder and keeps `daily/programmed challenge` on the cadence/editorial side unless a true event object is explicitly present.
- Whether supporter language stays decoupled from room fairness, guaranteed participation, and service-priority promises, and whether any benefit wording quietly selects `editorial/content`, `hosted convenience`, or `paid participation`.
- Whether hosting language preserves `trusted operators` as the first transition seam and keeps `official hosted convenience`, `promoted third-party hosts`, and support ownership visibly distinct.
- Whether contribution/discovery language keeps the official Prix-curated library as the trust anchor, preserves `guest editor / editorial activation` without canonizing it as a locked namespace, and keeps unsupported/operator-only surfaces sharply separated from the main library.
- Whether mastery, recap, group residue, event memory, and content/library history remain distinct ledgers in current planning prose, with `event memory` tested as an event-object seam rather than a ready-made UI.
- Whether preserve-only branches still have enough structural room to exist later without current docs accidentally foreclosing them.
- Whether reversal-sensitive propositions are being smuggled back in through softened terms such as `premium`, `membership`, `community host`, `marketplace`, `featured event`, or `supporter access`.

## What the distributed sensitivity pass must not resolve by force

- It must not pick a universal first-wrapper winner.
- It must not finalize the first bounded-audience bundle or treat stronger interactive audience rights as already chosen.
- It must not promote `editorial/content` from stronger general later default into committed later winner.
- It must not promote `official hosted convenience` from stronger service-identity branch into chosen roadmap commitment, and it must not demote `collectives` into a dead branch simply because they are preserve-only.
- It must not convert `guest editor / editorial activation` into finalized namespace doctrine, nor promote `partner brand / creator profile` into the default later identity.
- It must not activate `reviewed submissions`, `unsupported listings`, or `partner-brand discovery` by force simply to make the later contribution posture look cleaner.
- It must not treat `paid guaranteed participation`, `priority`, `membership`, or `support-plus-beta` as harmless deferred upsells.
- It must not choose the first mastery metric, exact cadence taxonomy, or achievement-visibility rule.
- It must not treat `featured challenge`, `race-weekend spotlight`, and `event memory` as interchangeable, or force a concrete event-memory UI before a real event object is chosen.
