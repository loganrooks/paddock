# 05 Gap Closure: Mature Product, Flywheel, Community, And Premium Surfaces

## Why this exists

The current audit chain closed the architecture-foreclosure question strongly for:

- room and topology
- visibility and reveal
- authority and lifecycle
- identity, memory, and cadence layering

But the closeout verification correctly left one major thread only partially answered:

- mature product shape
- content flywheel
- retention loop
- monetization / premium posture
- community and streamer/spectator surfaces

That means the current sensitivity work is incomplete by construction. It cannot propagate implications from a product-shape thread that was never explored to the required depth.

This gap-closure round exists so the eventual Phase 01 rerun consumes *fully updated canon*, not just the subset of the exploratory audit that reached doctrine-quality synthesis first.

## Core correction

Do not treat this as "one more ideation round."

Treat it as:

1. a targeted closure pass on a named open gap from the audit verifier
2. a dependency for any final rerun-input canon patch
3. a prerequisite to deciding whether the current `LONG-ARC.md`, `PROJECT.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `REQUIREMENTS.md`, and `01-CONTEXT.md` are complete enough for a fresh Phase 01 discuss rerun

## What is missing today

The handoff explicitly asked for richer answers on:

- mature product vision
- content-flywheel design
- retention mechanics
- monetization architecture
- streamer growth shape
- community features beyond gameplay

The current audit trail improved experience mapping and architecture exposure, but never gave this thread an equally rigorous dedicated round. As a result:

- `whole-job-verification-output.md` still marks this thread as materially open
- `LONG-ARC.md` and related canon may still be under-sensitive to these future pressures
- the current rerun boundary for Phase 01 is still provisional rather than final

## What the first delegated pass should do

This first pass is **context + research-plan + task-shaping**, not final closure.

It should:

- gather and synthesize the best current local context around the mature-product gap
- identify exactly which sub-questions are load-bearing enough to affect canon or rerun inputs
- distinguish what already exists in current docs from what is truly underexplored
- propose the smallest good next-round structure to close the gap properly
- explicitly state whether the closure needs:
  - one substantive lane
  - multiple parallel lanes plus synthesis
  - external reference research in addition to local deliberation
  - a follow-on sensitivity pass after substantive exploration

## Questions this pass must answer

1. Which parts of the mature-product thread are already answered well enough in existing canon and audit outputs?
2. Which parts are actually still missing, and why are they missing?
3. Which missing parts are likely to matter for:
   - `PROJECT.md`
   - `LONG-ARC.md`
   - `ROADMAP.md`
   - `REQUIREMENTS.md`
   - future Phase 01 steering
4. What is the right closure shape:
   - one lane
   - several lanes
   - local-only deliberation
   - local + web research
5. What exact downstream artifact chain should follow this pass?

## Two kinds of gap this pass must distinguish

Do not only ask whether the audit is incomplete relative to the original handoff/request.

Also ask whether the audit itself, through its own exploration and synthesis, has exposed additional load-bearing gaps that were not named crisply in the original handoff.

So the output must distinguish:

- `handoff-relative gaps`
  things the original handoff/request asked for that remain underanswered
- `emergent audit-revealed gaps`
  things the audit uncovered as important, but which were not fully specified at the start

The gap-closure recommendation should speak to both.

For clarity:

- this first pass may analyze both together at the context-mapping level
- but the recommended **substantive closure work** should assume they are likely separate downstream tasks or lanes unless the evidence strongly shows one combined lane is cleaner

## Required output

Write one artifact:

- `05-gap-closure-context-and-plan.md`

That artifact must include:

- a short status judgment on the maturity of the current canon with respect to this gap
- a map of sub-gaps under the broad mature-product thread
- a distinction between `handoff-relative gaps` and `emergent audit-revealed gaps`
- evidence/provenance for each sub-gap
- a recommendation for the next execution shape
  - explicitly say whether the downstream closure should split into:
    - `handoff-gap closure`
    - `emergent-gap closure`
  - if not, justify why a combined substantive round is still better
- a dependency chain showing what has to happen before the Phase 01 discuss rerun
- explicit notes on whether a new sensitivity pass is required after substantive closure

## Important guardrails

- Do not pretend the existing sensitivity map already closes this thread.
- Do not collapse "mature product" into only monetization.
- Do not collapse "community" into only public social features; private folklore, recurrence, and bounded audience shells may be more important.
- Do not widen this into a full business plan unless the evidence genuinely forces that.
- Do not patch canon yet in this first pass. This pass should shape the closure work, not skip directly to doctrinal edits.
