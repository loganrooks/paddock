Why these belong together:

- the remediation contribution packet already rejected open marketplace/public publishing as an active branch for now
- the residual problem is the rough shape of later approved outside-pack futures and where discovery/unsupported listing surfaces would live if ever activated
- those questions materially affect endorsement, support language, and product identity

This chunk is not trying to close:

- partner brands
- guest editors
- Prix-curated copies

- listed-but-unsupported admin/self-host surfaces

## Focus

- where unsupported listings would live
- whether future approved outside packs appear as brands, editors, curated copies, or something else
- how discovery differs between core product, admin surfaces, and self-host contexts
