# 05 Sensitivity Docket D2: Discovery And Unsupported Listings Output

## 1. Docket framing

- Mode: `feature-to-doc map`
- Classification: `initial architecture research/planning`
- Purpose: translate the current discovery and listing posture into doc-level sensitivity rules without promoting reviewed submissions, unsupported listings, or marketplace language into active product doctrine.
- This docket does not choose a future discovery model.
- It exists to keep the canon from accidentally turning `later contribution/discovery seam exists` into `guest-facing creator-platform already exists`.

## 2. Target feature pressure

- reviewed submissions stay preserve-only
- unsupported listings stay outside the main guest-facing library
- marketplace/public publishing remains reversal-sensitive
- the official Prix-curated library remains the main trust anchor for any guest-facing discovery language

## 3. Current gate classification

- `direct doctrine`
  - the official Prix-curated library remains the main trust anchor
  - unsupported listings do not belong in the main guest-facing library
  - creator-forward discovery is not a first-value default for the current private-first product center
- `preserve-only`
  - reviewed submissions as a later intake seam
  - a segregated operator/admin/self-host listing surface for unsupported additions
- `bounded-open`
  - whether reviewed submissions later feed only editorial activation or also a segregated unsupported/operator catalog
  - the exact future wording and metadata ladder between `reviewed`, `approved`, `featured`, `curated`, and `unsupported`
- `reversal-sensitive`
  - `marketplace`, `public publishing`, or `creator catalog` language re-entering by softer wording
  - unsupported listings moving into the main guest-facing library
  - review-state language being used as if it already proves endorsement, durability, or support ownership

## 4. Current survivor and strongest rival(s)

- Current survivor:
  - official curated discovery stays Prix-first
  - approved outside packs, if they ever become guest-facing, still inherit the official library as the trust anchor rather than a separate creator-discovery surface
  - unsupported listings survive only as a narrow operator/admin/self-host seam
- Strongest rival pressures:
  - `reviewed submissions` drifting from preserve-only intake language into an implied guest-facing browse surface
  - `partner brand / creator profile` and broader creator-forward discovery being smuggled back in through `pack sharing`, `discover`, or `community` wording
  - `marketplace/public publishing` re-entering through loose language around pack libraries, outside packs, or review queues

## 5. Why this pressure matters

- The live canon already preserves later content operations, pack sharing, reusable content history, and explicit visibility stages.
- That is enough to keep future discovery seams alive, but it is also enough to create drift if `sharing`, `library`, `browse`, `reviewed`, and `public discovery` start reading like one ladder.
- The main risk here is not lack of future awareness. It is false equivalence:
  - `reviewed` can mean queue-processed rather than endorsed
  - `approved` can mean allowed rather than curated
  - `curated` can mean official Prix activation
  - `unsupported` can mean intentionally segregated rather than lightly official
- If those terms blur, the docs will quietly import:
  - creator-platform assumptions
  - stronger endorsement inference
  - stronger support and moderation expectations
  - a guest-facing library that no longer matches the private-first trust posture

## 6. Relevant planning surfaces

- `.planning/PROJECT.md`

- `.planning/LONG-ARC.md`
  - discovery posture, contribution/sharing doctrine, protected bets, and explicit deferrals
- `.planning/ROADMAP.md`
  - visibility language, Phase 6 curation/calibration wording, and future wrapper/library preservation
- `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`
  - curated-pack baseline, seam language, visibility/access separation, and explicit deferrals around public publishing

## 7. Per-doc implication map

### [PROJECT.md](/home/rookslog/workspace/projects/prix-guesser/.planning/PROJECT.md)

- Safe:
  - keep the product center explicitly private-first and not ambient-public-discovery-first
  - preserve later `pack sharing`, richer content operations, and reusable content/history surfaces as future-aware seam language
  - keep broader public discovery as a deliberate later obligation shift rather than a default continuation of Milestone 2
- Overcommit risk:
  - if `pack sharing` or `richer content operations` starts to read like guest-facing creator browse, reviewed-submission intake, or public pack discovery is already part of the expected arc
  - if `broader public discovery` starts to read like a neutral scaling step instead of an obligation-bearing product change
- Silent foreclosure risk:
  - if the high-level story leaves room only for official internal curation and erases the preserve-only seam for reviewed submissions or operator-only unsupported listings
  - if later outside-pack possibilities are described only as public creator discovery, making the segregated operator/admin path disappear
- What must remain explicit here:
  - the official curated library is still the guest-facing trust anchor
  - reviewed submissions are not a guest-facing discovery commitment
  - unsupported listings, if they exist later, are outside the main library

### [LONG-ARC.md](/home/rookslog/workspace/projects/prix-guesser/.planning/LONG-ARC.md)

- Safe:
  - keep `trusted private access can exist without public discovery`
  - keep `contribution and sharing questions should be staged carefully`
  - keep `open creator marketplace or broad public publishing` deferred
  - keep broad creator ecosystems as separate future decisions rather than a present-tense identity drift
- Overcommit risk:
  - if contribution/sharing doctrine starts sounding like eventual public creator browse is the natural next layer
  - if later streamer/spectator or broader ecosystem language gets read as proof that creator-facing discovery is already part of the intended product identity
- Silent foreclosure risk:
  - if the long-arc doctrine is read as `only official curation exists forever`, leaving no bounded preserve-only seam for reviewed submissions or segregated unsupported/operator discovery
  - if support, endorsement, and discovery are flattened into one axis
- What must remain explicit here:
  - discovery posture, support posture, and endorsement posture are separate
  - later discovery beyond the official library, if ever allowed, must be explicitly segregated and obligation-aware
  - `marketplace/public publishing` remains a reversal-sensitive change, not a soft synonym for later outside contribution

### [ROADMAP.md](/home/rookslog/workspace/projects/prix-guesser/.planning/ROADMAP.md)

- Safe:
  - keep v1 and Phase 6 anchored on curated starter packs, calibration, and reusable content seams
  - keep future content-history and wrapper reuse language generic rather than discovery-model-specific
  - keep broader public discovery as a later deliberate branch rather than a roadmap assumption
- Overcommit risk:
  - if Phase 6 curation/calibration language starts to imply future reviewed-submission intake, public pack browse, or unsupported listing surfaces in the main app
  - if roadmap references to broader pack libraries or other wrappers start to sound like `marketplace later` by default
- Silent foreclosure risk:
  - if roadmap wording preserves only official curated starter content and leaves no future seam for trusted-private sharing, reviewed-submission intake, or operator-only unsupported catalogs
  - if unsupported/operator discovery disappears because all later content language stays guest-facing by default
- What must remain explicit here:
  - the roadmap currently commits only to curated official starter packs
  - preserving later content/library seams does not choose a guest-facing discovery model
  - unsupported listings remain off the main guest-facing path even as broader pack/history seams are preserved

### [REQUIREMENTS.md](/home/rookslog/workspace/projects/prix-guesser/.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md)

- Safe:
  - keep `PACK-01`, `PACK-05`, and `ROOM-01` anchored on curated packs
  - let `SEAM-04` and `SEAM-05` preserve future content/wrapper reuse and visibility/access separation
  - let `DEF-05` and the out-of-scope `Broad public UGC publishing or pack marketplace` rule carry the non-commitment load
- Overcommit risk:
  - if future requirements start naming `reviewed packs`, `community packs`, `creator pages`, or public browse/discover features as though they are already doctrine-ready extensions of the current pack model
  - if seam language is treated as proof that future public publishing or guest-facing unsupported discovery is expected
- Silent foreclosure risk:
  - if the requirement surface becomes so tightly official-only that later preserve-only reviewed-submission or operator-catalog seams would require a doctrine rewrite instead of fitting inside the preserved architecture
  - if `curated` is used as though it already settles all future status vocabulary
- What must remain explicit here:
  - curated packs are the active baseline, not a full taxonomy of every future pack status
  - visibility and access seams are preserved without promoting public discovery
  - later discovery states, if added, must distinguish `reviewed`, `approved`, `curated`, and `unsupported` rather than collapsing them

## 8. What each doc may safely imply

- the official Prix-curated library is the default guest-facing discovery surface
- later pack sharing, richer content operations, and reusable content/history surfaces may exist without implying a creator-platform turn
- reviewed submissions may remain a preserved future intake seam without becoming active guest-facing browse doctrine
- unsupported listings may remain a preserved later seam only when they are clearly segregated into operator/admin/self-host surfaces
- broader public discovery may be named only as a deliberate obligation-bearing later decision, not as a neutral extension of current pack growth

## 9. What each doc must not imply

- that reviewed submissions are already close to guest-facing activation
- that unsupported or third-party listings belong beside official packs in the main guest-facing library
- that `reviewed`, `approved`, `featured`, and `curated` are interchangeable product states
- that `pack sharing`, `discover`, `community`, or `library growth` naturally imply creator pages, public browse, or marketplace posture
- that `marketplace`, `public publishing`, or broad creator discovery is the default long-arc destination

## 10. What must remain explicit

- the official curated library remains the trust anchor
- reviewed submissions stay preserve-only
- unsupported listings stay outside the main guest-facing library
- discovery posture, endorsement posture, and support posture are separate questions
- `marketplace/public publishing` remains reversal-sensitive and cannot re-enter through softer discovery language
- later outside-pack discovery, if it ever exists, needs explicit segregation and status language rather than casual carry-forward wording

## 11. Semantic drift watchpoints

- `reviewed`
  - should mean queue-processed or decisioned, not automatically endorsed, durable, or guest-facing
- `approved`
  - should not be used as if it automatically means officially curated
- `curated`
  - should stay reserved for official Prix-controlled activation into the main trust surface
- `unsupported`
  - should imply explicit separation, risk/support limits, and non-default placement
- `pack sharing`, `discover`, `browse`, `library`
  - these can preserve future seams, but they can also quietly import creator-platform expectations if left unqualified
- `creator`, `community`, `marketplace`
  - these are not neutral synonyms for later outside contribution
- `public`
  - must stay distinct across `unlisted/share-by-link`, broader public discovery, and open public publishing

## 12. Sensitivity lane routing

- `Lane I: canon doctrine`
  - primary consumer for `PROJECT.md`, `LONG-ARC.md`, and `REQUIREMENTS.md`
  - test whether discovery wording preserves the official trust anchor without importing creator-platform semantics
- `Lane II: roadmap and rerun`
  - primary consumer for `ROADMAP.md`
  - test whether curation/calibration and later-content seams stay neutral about discovery model and keep unsupported listings off the guest-facing spine
- `Lane III: preserve-only and reversal-sensitive futures`
  - primary consumer for this docket's core burden
  - protect reviewed submissions as preserve-only
  - protect unsupported/operator listings as segregated
  - flag `marketplace/public publishing` or guest-facing unsupported-listing drift immediately
- `Lane IV: inquiry debt and anti-hard-coding`
  - inspect the still-open routing question around whether reviewed submissions, if ever used, feed editorial activation only or also a segregated operator catalog
  - inspect status-vocabulary drift between `reviewed`, `approved`, `curated`, and `unsupported`

## 13. What remains open after this docket

- whether reviewed submissions, if ever activated, feed only official editorial activation or also a segregated operator/admin catalog
- the exact future metadata and label ladder for `reviewed`, `approved`, `featured`, `curated`, and `unsupported`
- the exact operator/admin/self-host surface where unsupported listings would live if that seam is ever exercised
- whether any later solo or public-light wrapper ever earns stronger creator-forward browse; that remains adjacent pressure from D1, not settled doctrine here
- how later trusted-private sharing and later discovery should relate without collapsing into public publishing or creator-platform language
