# Round 2B Lane Common Scaffold

## Shared framing

Round 2B lanes are not new ideation lanes.

They are pressure-mapping and planning-surface lanes.

They must work from:

- the experience map already produced in Round 2A
- the engineering/scaling research already produced in `lane-i` and `lane-j`

## Shared traceability contract

Every Round 2B lane output must:

- state which `RESP-*` and `GAP-*` items it is helping answer
- identify which sources are steering vs background-only
- distinguish inherited findings from new synthesis
- say explicitly what it is not answering
- leave `RESP-04` closure claims to the designated synthesis/ledger stage unless the lane is itself the Wave 2 ledger lane
- map at least provisional downstream ripple across planning surfaces where relevant

## Shared calibration carry-forward

Every lane must preserve these live methodological constraints:

- do not replace product experience with architecture jargon
- do not presume one universal ontology or one universal room shape
- do not force all modes to support all contexts
- do not overstate technical confidence from suggestive references
- do not treat one reference product's mechanism as a direct blueprint
- do not let creator examples harden into taxonomies unless the artifact justifies that move
- treat `HANDOFF.md` as question-source and original pressure, not as binding solution authority
- do not assume the relevant planning surface is only `Phase 01`; some findings may ripple across Milestone 01 or canon docs

## Stale inheritance rule

Background-only historical artifacts remain:

- `01-round-1/round-1-self-eval.md`
- `03-next-round/round-2-prompts.md`

They may be mentioned when historically useful, but they should not override:

- `00-governance/review-trail-framework.md`
- `00-governance/next-round-gap-review.md`
- `03-next-round/round-2a-experience-archetypes-output.md`
- `02-lanes/architecture/lane-i-output.md`
- `02-lanes/architecture/lane-j-output.md`
- the lane's own task spec

## Required output sections

Each Round 2B lane output should include:

1. `Lane framing`
2. `Traceability and scope`
3. `Calibration carry-forward`
4. `Path of inquiry`
5. `Pressure map`
6. `Candidate early decisions or seams`
7. `Shortcuts likely to cause foreclosure`
8. `Artifact and planning-surface ripple map`
9. `Open uncertainties and evidence quality`
10. `Coverage note`

For `Wave 1` lanes specifically:

- any `Candidate early decisions or seams` must be labeled provisional pressure signals
- `Wave 1` lanes must not classify anything into `explicit now`, `keep open`, or `defer`
- that classification belongs to `Wave 2` and the final synthesis

## Output tone

The outputs should be:

- concrete
- planning-facing
- explicit about uncertainty
- comparative where needed

They should not read like:

- generic architecture essays
- framework worship
- stack recommendation documents
