# Round 2A Lane A Task Spec

## Lane purpose

This lane explores the strongest forms of:

- local recurring party night
- couch / shared-stage play
- host-screen plus private-device local play
- unconventional local forms such as partial separation, separate rooms, or same-house asymmetric setups

## Traceability and scope

This lane:

- directly supports `RESP-03`
- most strongly informs `GAP-03`, `GAP-04`, and `GAP-07`
- contributes evidence toward `RESP-04`
- does not itself close `RESP-04`

Background-only historical artifacts:

- `01-round-1/round-1-self-eval.md`
- `03-next-round/round-2-prompts.md`

## Calibration carry-forward

Preserve these corrections locally:

- treat ideas as design terrain, not fixed pitches
- do not force one ontology across all games
- do not convert creator examples into mandatory categories
- preserve room for unconventional local forms to matter on their own terms
- keep the focus on lived experience, not family ranking

## Core question

What kinds of Prix Guesser experiences are most alive when people are together locally, and what local-only or local-first design pressures appear there?

## Required lenses

- room energy
- reveal cadence
- host performance
- embarrassment tolerance
- mixed-knowledge social play
- recurring group memory
- what gets better or worse when players are partially separated even though still local

## Required questions

- what actually makes a great local Prix Guesser night feel distinct?
- which game territories become most alive under local co-presence?
- what experiences only work, or work best, locally?
- which ideas become stronger under host-screen plus phones or partial separation?
- where does local play want spectacle, pacing, shame-buffering, or performer energy?
- what local forms should be considered central versus niche but valuable?

## Anti-goals

Do not:

- reduce this lane to generic "couch multiplayer is good"
- treat remote play as the main comparison axis
- turn unconventional local forms into side notes
- solve solo async or public community problems here

## Required output sections

1. `Lane framing`
2. `Traceability and scope`
3. `Calibration carry-forward`
4. `What this experience feels like in practice`
5. `What kinds of game territories fit or resist it`
6. `What this experience wants from topology, authority, visibility, and cadence`
7. `What this experience does to privacy, recurrence, and community`
8. `What this lane changes about existing game judgments`
9. `What remains uncertain`
10. `What to carry into foreclosure synthesis`
11. `Coverage note`
