# Round 2A Lane C Task Spec

## Lane purpose

This lane explores:

- solo async play
- daily and weekly return shapes
- race-weekend ritual
- repeat cadence outside the live group session

## Traceability and scope

This lane:

- directly supports `RESP-03`
- most strongly informs `GAP-03`, `GAP-04`, and `GAP-07`
- contributes evidence toward `RESP-04`
- does not itself close `RESP-04`

Background-only historical artifacts:

- `01-round-1/round-1-self-eval.md`
- `03-next-round/round-2-prompts.md`

## Calibration carry-forward

Preserve these corrections locally:

- do not treat async as just a watered-down social mode
- distinguish ritual from grind
- let F1 calendar texture matter where it genuinely changes the experience
- keep the focus on lived cadence, not abstract retention metrics alone

## Core question

What mature Prix Guesser experiences work when a player returns alone, asynchronously, and repeatedly over time?

## Required lenses

- ritual and cadence
- retention without social co-presence
- race-calendar timing
- editorial/content cadence
- progression and collection
- recurrence without grind

## Required questions

- what makes solo async Prix Guesser feel alive rather than like generic quiz churn?
- what should be tied to the F1 calendar and what should be evergreen?
- which existing game territories can sustain solo async derivatives without losing their identity?
- what kinds of progression or collection feel right here?
- what kinds of async experiences are central versus merely plausible?
- where does async create a genuinely different good rather than a compromised shadow of live play?

## Anti-goals

Do not:

- drift into public/community shell questions
- treat solo async as only a monetization or metrics problem
- assume every social mode needs a solo derivative
- solve architecture foreclosure directly here
