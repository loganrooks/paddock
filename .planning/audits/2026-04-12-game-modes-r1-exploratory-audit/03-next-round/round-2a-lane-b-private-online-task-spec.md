# Round 2A Lane B Task Spec

## Lane purpose

This lane explores:

- private online synchronous groups
- Discord-group play
- hidden-info remote play
- topology-sensitive remote or separated-local forms

## Traceability and scope

This lane:

- directly supports `RESP-03`
- most strongly informs `GAP-03`, `GAP-04`, and `GAP-07`
- contributes evidence toward `RESP-04`
- does not itself close `RESP-04`

Background-only historical artifacts:

- `01-round-1/round-1-self-eval.md`
- `03-next-round/round-2-prompts.md`

Routing rule for overlap with Lane A:

- if the case is mainly about co-present room energy, host timing, or shared-stage spectacle, it belongs in Lane A
- if the case is mainly about private views, secrecy, comms, trust, or remote-like topology, it belongs here

## Calibration carry-forward

Preserve these corrections locally:

- remote play is not just lesser couch play
- examples can be probes rather than taxonomies
- asymmetric or topology-sensitive forms should not be flattened into generic online categories
- lived interaction shape matters more than feature-list thinking

## Core question

What kinds of Prix Guesser experiences become strong, weak, or transformed when the social group is synchronous but not co-present?

## Required lenses

- comms bandwidth
- trust and privacy
- role asymmetry
- hidden information
- shared versus private screens
- friction of remote coordination
- what separated-local play inherits from remote topology

## Required questions

- what social pleasures survive remote sync well?
- what pleasures need compensation when faces and physical co-presence disappear?
- which existing game territories improve under private views and remote comms?
- what remote forms deserve to be treated as first-class rather than compromised versions of couch play?
- where do private-room trust and voice dynamics matter more than raw networking?
- which unconventional local forms really belong in this lane because their topology is remote-like?

## Anti-goals

Do not:

- turn this into a generic online-features memo
- drift into solo async retention logic
- drift into public/community platform design
- assume remote is just lesser couch play

## Required output sections

1. `Lane framing`
2. `Traceability and scope`
3. `Calibration carry-forward`
4. `What this experience feels like in practice`
5. `What kinds of game territories fit or resist it`
6. `What this experience wants from topology, authority, visibility, and cadence`
7. `What this experience does to privacy, recurrence, and community`
8. `What this lane changes about existing game judgments`
9. `What remains uncertain`
10. `What to carry into foreclosure synthesis`
11. `Coverage note`
