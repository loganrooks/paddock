# Round 1 Output

## Round 1 read

The main result of Round 1 is not one winning game. It is a clearer portfolio shape.

The current corpus already contains several genuinely promising families, but they are promising for different reasons and in different contexts. The strongest mistake we could make after this round would be to flatten them into one of these bad summaries:

- “these are the serious modes”
- “these are the meme modes”
- “these are the UGC modes”
- “these should all work in every context”

That would destroy the useful distinctions Round 1 actually surfaced.

## What looks strongest now

### 1. `Words of Wisdom`

This is the strongest authored-reveal family currently in the corpus.

Why:

- it already has a real social replay engine
- it already has concrete content depth
- it is mixed-skill friendly without becoming flat
- it can likely support a few different branches without losing identity

The important thing is not to reduce it to “radio Fibbage.” The live family surface is broader than that:

- the social Fibbage core
- audio-enhanced reveal variants
- eventized / appointment / async derivatives

### 2. `The Stewards' Room`

This is the strongest judgment-family candidate currently in the corpus.

Why:

- the reveal-and-argument loop is already visible
- the mode is F1-native without being trapped in trivia
- it can support both serious verdict play and louder social-identity court play

The key risk is packet design. It still lives or dies on how partial information is tuned.

### 3. `Paddock Fashion`

This is one of the strongest player-generated families in the corpus.

Why:

- it already has a reveal machine
- it already has multiple constraint systems
- it has a launchable low-asset version
- it naturally produces spectator energy

The key thing to preserve is multiplicity. It should not be flattened into “draw funny outfit.” It already wants multiple expressive forks.

### 4. `The Verstappen Game` / predator-hunter family

This is the strongest current action-family branch.

Why:

- it translates personality into mechanic instead of trivia flavor
- it already has real replay scaffolding
- it supports spectacle without being only spectacle
- it naturally accommodates unlock ladders, challenge modes, and rematch energy

This family should be preserved even if the public-facing names or tone shift later.

## What is promising but still under-shaped

These should stay alive, but with clearer expectations about their current maturity:

### `commentary / crowd recognition`

This looks like the strongest continuation of the current sound bundle.

What to preserve:

- cultural recognition
- spectator value
- eventized freshness

What not to do:

- keep pretending the entire current sound bundle is one coherent mode family

### `Team Principal's Desk`

This has a strong fantasy, but the current symmetric “pit / stay out” shell is still thin.

The most promising future versions are likely:

- asymmetric team-talk variants
- confidence / calibration variants
- more structured hindsight/strategy forks

### `The Grid Walk`

This should stay alive as a distinct branch, not as a side note under judgment modes.

Its value is that it may become one of the strongest `remote sync / discussion-first co-op` candidates in the whole corpus.

### `Meme Prompts`

This is promising, but still under-shaped.

It should not be discarded, but it also should not be treated as mature just because Jackbox-style references exist. The main question is whether it wants to become:

1. a stronger standalone hidden-discovery mode
2. a cluster of more structured descendants
3. mostly a private-pack social system

### `Pit Stop Co-op`

This is a strong microgame seed, not yet a fully developed family.

Its likely strength is not in trying to become the deepest branch in the portfolio, but in being a highly readable coordination spike with great short-session energy.

## What Round 1 changed structurally

### 1. Sound should be split

Round 1 does not support keeping `Sound-based Games` as one portfolio item.

At minimum it should be split into:

- `commentary / crowd recognition`
- `engine identification`

The first is a plausible future branch.
The second is still conditional on later research.

### 2. Lane B is not one “serious mode” bucket

The judgment/information lane actually contains at least three different pleasures:

- debate theater
- strategy hindsight
- asymmetric information co-op

Those should be compared separately in later rounds.

### 3. Lane D is really two families

The current Lane D bundle was good enough for Round 1, but synthesis should preserve this split:

- `Pit Stop Co-op` as a coordination microgame
- `Verstappen / Talib` as a predator-hunter family

### 4. Expression modes should stay private-first by default

The best current read is:

- private creation first
- curated public/community later

This is not a retreat from ambition. It is a better reading of what those modes are actually good at.

## Cross-portfolio judgments

### Not every mode needs every context

Round 1 strongly supports refusing the assumption that each mode should be optimized for local, sync, async, spectator, public community, and solo all at once.

The better question is:

- where is this mode naturally strong?
- where can it stretch?
- where would support feel forced?

### Virality and retention must stay separate

This is one of the most important cross-lane findings.

Some modes are already legible, funny, or clip-worthy.
That is not enough.

The better portfolio candidates are the ones that can pair a hook with a return loop:

- `Words of Wisdom`: curation + social fabrication
- `Paddock Fashion`: reveal machine + recurring folklore
- `Verstappen / Talib`: readable chaos + unlock/rematch structure
- `Stewards`: verdict reveal + argument loop

### Session role matters

Round 1 surfaced a distinction that should shape later rounds:

- some modes look like headliners
- some look like short spikes / palate cleansers
- some look like hidden-discovery side modes
- some look like remote specialists

This means later rounds should compare portfolio composition, not just isolated mode quality.

## New or sharpened proposals from Round 1

These are not all-new modes from nowhere. They are the strongest sharpened branches Round 1 surfaced:

- `Words of Wisdom` as a broader family:
  social Fibbage core + audio-enhanced variants + eventized async derivatives
- `commentary / crowd recognition` as its own sound branch
- `The Stewards' Room` with a split between serious verdict play and social-identity court play
- `Team Principal's Desk` as a likely asymmetric/confidence fork rather than a thin symmetric strategy prompt
- `The Grid Walk` as a distinct remote-sync cooperative-information branch
- `Paddock Fashion` as a family of expressive forks, not one fashion-drawing gimmick
- `Meme Prompts` descendants such as more structured contest/court/brief formats
- `Verstappen / Talib` as a preserved predator-hunter family with vehicle ladders, challenge modes, and circuit variation

## Things to park for later research

These should not be used to prematurely collapse Round 1 judgments:

- audio/legal viability around sound-heavy branches
- whether engine identification is actually acoustically viable
- moderation and discovery systems for public/community expression
- authored-content sourcing for reactive strategy/judgment branches
- hidden-information / room-model / control-surface implications
- performance/network/control burden for action-chaos branches

These are real later questions. They are not reasons to pretend the underlying design judgment cannot proceed.

## What I would carry forward most strongly

If Round 2 only preserved a few load-bearing judgments from this round, they should be these:

- keep the strong families open:
  `Words of Wisdom`, `Stewards`, `Paddock Fashion`, predator-hunter action
- do not treat all audio as one family
- do not flatten Lane B into one “serious” bucket
- do not flatten Lane C into one generic UGC bucket
- do not flatten Lane D into one generic chaos bucket
- keep `private folklore generation` and `session role` as real evaluative categories
- keep `virality hook` and `return loop` distinct in every later judgment
