| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-01 | T | `packages/content-tools/src/loadYaml.ts` | mitigate | Parse YAML with `strict: true` and `uniqueKeys: true`, and convert parser failures into blocking diagnostics before schema validation continues. |

| T-04-04 | D | `packages/content-tools/src/cli.ts` | mitigate | Keep validate/compile as explicit commands with exit-on-error behavior so high-severity authored-content failures block later phases instead of being logged and ignored. |
</threat_model>
