### Understanding Changed

The reruns did not reverse the earlier picture.

They mostly:

- made several tensions less neat
- forced sharper distinctions in the language we use

The most important sharpenings were:

- `wrapper`, `stage-shape`, `sibling product`, and `cross-cutting spectator branch` should not be collapsed into one term
- the likely first strong proof still looks like a private, watchable, host-led ritual
- solo and async still look more like extension paths than the emotional center

- feasibility must be treated as plural:
  - technical
  - operational
  - adoption
  - economic
  - ethical / trust
- hosting transition is not just a scale story; it is also an expectations, accountability, observability, and trust story
