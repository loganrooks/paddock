## What Actually Happened

All four exploratory research lanes eventually landed:

- `01-product-futures`
- `02-hosting-transition`
- `03-precedents-and-trajectories`
- `04-future-aware-planning`

At the same time, the user reviewed the checkpointing approach itself and criticized it as too brief and too lossy. The user specifically pointed out that the checkpoints were flattening the conversation too much and preserving the wrong details.

That critique is important because it is itself part of the exploration design. The continuity system is not just passive logging; it shapes what future selves will remember as significant.

## What The User Was Pushing On

The user’s critique was not just "write more words."

It was:

- preserve the nuance of the back-and-forth
- preserve how proposals were challenged and reframed
- preserve the interpretive movement of the discussion
- stop centering low-value orchestration detail in the conversational checkpoints

The user explicitly distinguished between:

- details relevant to the exploration itself
- and details like research-agent deployment issues, which belong elsewhere unless they changed the meaning of the discussion

## Why This Matters

This feedback sharpens the continuity model in an important way.

An exploration checkpoint should not read like a project management status note. It should read like a disciplined memory of an evolving philosophical and product conversation.

That means it needs to preserve:

- conceptual moves
- disagreements
- reframings
- new distinctions introduced
- tensions that remained alive

not merely:

- topic headings
- completed actions
- generic summaries

## Research State At This Point

The research wave is now complete, and it contributes four meaningful strands:

### Product-futures lane

Strengthened the idea that Prix Guesser is better understood as multiple wrappers around one authored F1 substrate than as a forced choice between local party and solo/async.

### Hosting-transition lane

Strengthened the operational plausibility of a web-first staged path:

- LAN-first local play
- self-hosted remote play from personal hardware
- later small hosted/public operation

while emphasizing that the real early constraints are trust, reachability, operator burden, reconnect quality, and media delivery, not abstract "scale" alone.

### Precedents-and-trajectories lane

Sharpened which analogies are useful and which are misleading:

- GeoGuessr is useful for wrapper expansion around a core loop
- Jackbox is useful for host-screen/controller ritual and audience design
- Kahoot is useful for live-vs-async wrapper separation and operational clarity

but none of them should be copied wholesale.

### Future-aware-planning lane

Argued that the harness already contains promising future-aware behavior, but it needs to be made more systematic and more canonical through low-drag fields rather than heavier process.

## Shared Understanding At This Point

By now, the exploration has enough support to say several things with higher confidence:

- Prix Guesser should be treated as a staged product
- web-first remains plausible without excluding local-only or self-hosted paths
- local party play is still likely the first strong proof shape
- solo/async should probably be thought of as wrappers or support shells unless later evidence changes that
- watchability is multifaceted and should not be treated as one vague aesthetic term
- future-aware planning should become more explicit, but likely through lightweight schema rather than heavy governance

## What Is Still Open

Even with all research landed, the most important questions remain live:

1. What is the right mature end-state framing for the product?
2. What is the exact relationship between local party play, solo practice, async challenge play, and remote live rooms?
3. What should count as the shared substrate that all these wrappers depend on?
4. Which future possibilities deserve early protection, and which should remain consciously unprotected for now?
5. What kind of public transition and community-support model, if any, would remain aligned with the spirit of the project?

## What Changed In The Continuity Method

This checkpoint marks a methodological correction:

- future checkpoints should be more interpretive and more detailed
- they should preserve the actual argumentative texture of the conversation
- orchestration details should be linked, not foregrounded, unless they materially changed the exploration
